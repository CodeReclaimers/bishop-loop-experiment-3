## Qwen-Bishop expansion sweep completed; control question answered (commit 7a22c8f)

### Summary

Completed the 7-seed expansion of the qwen-1.5B Bishop control (seeds
4004-4010, both `bare_faithful` and `steelman` conditions, 90 min budget
each, ~21h wall-clock total). Combined with seeds 4001-4003, this brought
qwen-Bishop to balanced n=10 against the existing n=10 nemotron-4B Bishop
sweep, both under SEARCH/REPLACE bishop-arm application. The open question
from the earlier 3-seed control — "is steelman bishop-wins higher with the
smaller Bishop (53% qwen vs 35% nemotron) a real effect or noise?" — now
has a definitive answer.

### Verbatim verification evidence

Final comparison at n=10 vs n=10 (computed via `phase/analyze_results.py`
+ a follow-up scipy script):

```
condition          Bishop           n      best mean ± std    bishop wins
----------------------------------------------------------------------
bare_faithful      qwen-1.5B       10 0.0101 ± 0.0006      26/ 51 (51.0%)
bare_faithful      nemotron-4B     10 0.0101 ± 0.0006      25/ 42 (59.5%)
steelman           qwen-1.5B       10 0.0103 ± 0.0003      17/ 42 (40.5%)
steelman           nemotron-4B     10 0.0105 ± 0.0003      12/ 34 (35.3%)

=== Mann-Whitney U (final metric) ===
  bare_faithful      qwen vs nemo  U= 46.0 p=0.791
  steelman           qwen vs nemo  U= 38.0 p=0.385

=== Fisher exact (bishop arm wins proportion) ===
  bare_faithful      qwen[26/51] vs nemo[25/42]  odds_ratio=0.71 p=0.530
  steelman           qwen[17/42] vs nemo[12/34]  odds_ratio=1.25 p=0.813
```

All four pairwise tests give p ≥ 0.385. The two Bishop models are
indistinguishable on this experimental design.

### Sweep recovery (GPU driver issue)

The expansion was originally launched on 2026-05-22 20:29 but ran on CPU
only — `nvidia-smi` reported `Driver/library version mismatch (NVML
library version: 580.159)` after a system update, and Ollama silently
offloaded Skippy (22 GB) to CPU. Iteration time was 23× slower (8 iters
in 90 min vs the normal ~250). I killed the run after the first summary
fired, moved `bare_faithful_4004` aside to
`phase/results/bare_faithful_4004.cpu-only-2026-05-22/`, and asked the
user to reboot. After the reboot (2026-05-23 morning), Skippy was back on
GPU at 22.1 GB and Bishop at 2.8 GB; relaunched at 08:50 with
`--skip-existing` (so completed seeds wouldn't re-run) and the sweep
completed normally by 2026-05-24 05:54.

To prevent silent recurrence, the 4-hour cron status check
(`084c6b80`, now cancelled) was updated to verify Skippy's `size_vram > 0`
each fire — a driver fallback would now surface within 4 hours rather
than after one full 90-minute run.
