Replace the per‑item dict reassignment in `_parse_object` by accumulating `(k, v)` tuples in a list and assigning `out.update(dict(pairs))` after the loop, so the dictionary is built once instead of repeatedly rebuilding it. This eliminates the costly `out = dict(out); out[k] = v` pattern and reduces allocation churn. The same principle applies to `_parse_array`, where a list is built once and never rebuilt.
