To improve performance without sacrificing correctness, one effective change is to use a more efficient way to skip whitespace characters in the `_skip_ws` function. Currently, we use `text[pos:].lstrip(' \t\n\r')`, which requires scanning the entire string from position `pos` onward. This approach can be replaced with a generator expression that iterates over the string character by character until it encounters a non-whitespace character. This reduces the time complexity of skipping whitespace to O(n), where n is the length of the string, without compromising correctness.
