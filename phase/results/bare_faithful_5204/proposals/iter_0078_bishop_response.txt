One potential optimization is to use a stack-based approach for parsing objects, reducing the overhead of repeatedly building lists and dictionaries in the loop. This can be achieved by initializing an empty list or dictionary at each level of recursion and updating it as needed. This way, when a closing brace is encountered, we can pop elements from the stack and construct the object accordingly. The time complexity remains O(n) for parsing the string, but the space complexity decreases to O(max depth of the JSON structure).
