Replace the linear‑scan `_skip_ws` that loops over each whitespace character with a single call to `text.find` to locate the next non‑whitespace character, removing the loop and the surrounding try/except block. This change skips all whitespace in constant time instead of O(k) where k is the number of skipped characters, and eliminates the need for manual index incrementing. The rest of the parser can keep tracking the position with `pos = next_non_ws` or `pos = len(text)`.
