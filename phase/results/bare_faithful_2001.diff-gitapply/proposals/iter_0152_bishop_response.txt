Pre‑process the input at the start of `parse` by stripping all whitespace once with a compiled regular expression: `text = re.sub(r'\s+', '', text)`. After that, remove the `_skip_ws` calls and the per‑character whitespace loop, letting the parser work directly on the whitespace‑free string. This eliminates the O(n) loop and the repeated index checks, yielding a noticeable speedup while preserving correctness.
