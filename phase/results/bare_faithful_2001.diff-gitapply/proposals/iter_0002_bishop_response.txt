Replace the per‑call `_skip_ws` loop with a single call to `text.find('\\S', pos)` (or `text.find(next(_WS_CHARS), pos)`), which jumps directly to the next non‑whitespace character. This eliminates the inner while loop that examined each whitespace character individually and reduces the work from O(k) per skip to O(1) per skip, speeding up whitespace skipping throughout the parser.
