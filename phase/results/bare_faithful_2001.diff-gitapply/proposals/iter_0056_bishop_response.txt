Replace the incremental dictionary construction inside `_parse_object` with a simple list of key‑value pairs collected as you iterate, then at the end return `dict(zip(keys, values))` instead of repeatedly appending to a dict. This eliminates the O(n²) cost of repeated `out = out + [v]` and `out[key] = v` assignments, allowing the final `dict(zip(...))` to create the result in linear time while preserving correctness.
