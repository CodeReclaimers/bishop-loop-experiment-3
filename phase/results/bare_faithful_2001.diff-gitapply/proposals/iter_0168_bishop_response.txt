Remove the per‑call `_skip_ws` function and its calls throughout `parse` and `_parse_value`, and replace whitespace skipping with a single regex substitution that removes all whitespace characters before the parser runs. This eliminates the Python loop that checks each character and jumps ahead of it, letting the fast compiled regex handle the work in one pass. The rest of the parser can stay unchanged, preserving correctness and the `JSONParseError` interface.
