CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace character-by-character whitespace skipping with a regex is fundamentally flawed because it misunderstands the complexity analysis — skipping whitespace still requires examining each character in the input, so the complexity remains O(n). Additionally, regex matching has overhead that makes it slower than direct character lookups for simple cases. The approach also doesn't address the actual bottleneck, which is the repeated calls to `_skip_ws` in tight parsing loops.
