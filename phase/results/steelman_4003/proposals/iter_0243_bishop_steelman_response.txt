CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.strip()` for whitespace skipping is fundamentally flawed because `str.strip()` is designed to remove characters from both ends of a string, not to advance a position pointer through the string. Additionally, `str.strip()` doesn't handle the case where whitespace is interspersed with non-whitespace content — it removes all leading/trailing whitespace, not just the leading whitespace at a specific position.
