To optimize the `json_parser.py` script for parsing JSON, a common optimization is to skip whitespace characters during parsing. This approach reduces the amount of work required for each character scan and can lead to faster execution time for large datasets. One way to implement this is by using a generator function that yields characters from the string one at a time, skipping any leading whitespace characters before yielding the next character. This allows for immediate consumption of the whitespace during parsing rather than accumulating it in memory.
