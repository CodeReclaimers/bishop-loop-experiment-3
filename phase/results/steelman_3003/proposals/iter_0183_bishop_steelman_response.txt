CRITIQUE: The suggestion to remove the compiled regex `_WS_RE` and rely solely on `_skip_ws` is unworkable because `_skip_ws` processes whitespace character-by-character, which is inefficient for large amounts of consecutive whitespace. This approach would lead to O(n) time complexity for skipping n consecutive whitespace characters, whereas the regex can skip them in O(1) time. Additionally, the regex approach is more idiomatic and readable for this use case.
STEELMAN: The steelman is to replace the regex-based whitespace skipping with a hybrid approach: use a loop to skip initial whitespace with the frozenset lookup, but for large contiguous whitespace sections, use a regex to skip the entire block in one operation, thereby combining the benefits of both methods.
