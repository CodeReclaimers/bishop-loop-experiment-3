CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace character-by-character whitespace skipping with a single regex match fails because `_WS_RE` only matches zero or more whitespace characters at the start of the match, not the position after all leading whitespace. Using `re.match()` or `re.search()` would require repositioning the parser after the match, and the regex approach would not be truly O(1) since it still needs to advance the position by the matched length. Additionally, the regex engine's overhead could outweigh the benefits for small amounts of whitespace.
