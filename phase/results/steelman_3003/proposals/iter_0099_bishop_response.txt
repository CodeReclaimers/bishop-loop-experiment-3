Replace the current manual hex4 validation inside the Unicode escape parser with a single regex match for the full escape sequence, such as `re.fullmatch(r'\\u[0-9a-fA-F]{4}', text[pos:pos+5], 0)`. This lets the parser decode the entire escape in one operation and avoids the repeated `len` checks and `all` membership tests that ` _is_hex4` performs.
