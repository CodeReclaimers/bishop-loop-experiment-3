CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the char-by-char loop with a regex search would break the expected behavior because `_WS_RE.search(text, pos)` finds the *first* sequence of whitespace, not the *end* of the whitespace block. It would return the start of the whitespace run, not the position after it, leading to incorrect parsing. Additionally, the regex approach would be slower for short whitespace runs due to regex overhead.

STEELMAN: The steelman version would use `_WS_RE.match(text, pos)` to find the longest match of whitespace starting at `pos`, then advance the position by the match length. This preserves the performance benefits of regex while correctly returning the position after the whitespace block.
