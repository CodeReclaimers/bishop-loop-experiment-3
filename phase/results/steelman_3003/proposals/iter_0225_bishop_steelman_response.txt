CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while-loop in `_skip_ws` with a regex search would be incorrect because `re.search()` finds the *first* occurrence of whitespace, not the *end* of the whitespace sequence. This would skip only the first whitespace character instead of all leading whitespace, breaking the parser's ability to properly consume whitespace. Additionally, the regex approach would not be constant-time in practice due to the overhead of regex engine setup and matching, especially for small inputs where the simple loop is actually faster.
