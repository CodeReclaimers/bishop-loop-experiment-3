Replace the character‑by‑character whitespace‑skipping loop in `_skip_ws` with a single call to the compiled regex `_WS_RE.search(text, pos).start()`, which jumps directly to the next non‑whitespace character. This eliminates the per‑character loop and makes the function’s work O(1) instead of O(k) for k whitespace characters. Since all other parts already reuse `_skip_ws`, the parser benefits across the whole input.
