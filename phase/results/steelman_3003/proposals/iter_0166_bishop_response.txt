Replace the loop‑based `_skip_ws` that iterates character‑by‑character with a call to the compiled regular expression `_WS_RE.search(text, pos).end()`. This lets the regex engine skip all consecutive whitespace characters in one go, eliminating the per‑character loop. The change is limited to `_skip_ws` and preserves correctness because it still advances `pos` to the next non‑whitespace index.
