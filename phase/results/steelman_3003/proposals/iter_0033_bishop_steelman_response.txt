CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while-loop in `_skip_ws` with a regex search would fail because `re.search()` finds the *first* match, not the *entire* contiguous whitespace sequence starting at `pos`. This would leave trailing whitespace unprocessed, breaking the parser's state tracking. Additionally, regex-based skipping could be slower than the optimized character-by-character lookup for small amounts of whitespace.

STEELMAN: The steelman version uses `re.match()` instead of `re.search()` to match the entire leading whitespace sequence from `pos`, and returns `match.end()` to advance past it. This preserves the performance benefits of regex while maintaining correctness by ensuring all contiguous whitespace is consumed.
