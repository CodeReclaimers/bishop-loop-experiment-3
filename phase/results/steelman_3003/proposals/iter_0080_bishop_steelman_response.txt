CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while-loop in `_skip_ws` with a single `text.find` call is unworkable because `text.find` only finds the first occurrence of a character, not the first non-character in a set. Using `find` with a frozenset lookup would require iterating through the set, negating the benefit of O(1) lookup. Additionally, `text.find` doesn't support skipping multiple characters efficiently in one go, which is the core of the optimization.
