In `_parse_string` replace the series of repeated string concatenations (`out = out + c`, `out = out + "\f"` etc.) with an in‑memory list that collects the characters, then after the loop perform a single `''.join(list)` to build the final string, which eliminates the O(n²) cost of incremental concatenation. This change applies to all places inside `_parse_string` where a character is added to the output, keeping the parse logic and escape handling unchanged.
