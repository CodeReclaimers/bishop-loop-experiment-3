In the `_parse_string` method replace the repeated `out = out + c` (and the similar `out = out + "\f"` etc.) with a list that collects characters, then join the list once at the end; this eliminates the O(n²) cost of string concatenation and reduces the work to linear. The `_parse_object` already uses direct assignment `out[key] = value`, so no further rebuild is needed. This change speeds up parsing without altering the output format or correctness.
