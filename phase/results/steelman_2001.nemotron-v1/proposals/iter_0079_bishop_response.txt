In the `_parse_string` function replace every occurrence of building a string with repeated concatenation (`out = out + …`) by appending characters to a list buffer (`buf.append(ch)`) and, after the loop finishes, constructing the final string with `"".join(buf)`. This eliminates the O(n²) cost of in‑place string concatenation and yields a linear‑time string assembly without altering the parser’s correctness.
