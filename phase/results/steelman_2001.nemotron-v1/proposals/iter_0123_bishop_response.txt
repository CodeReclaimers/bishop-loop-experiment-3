In `_parse_string`, replace every repeated string concatenation such as `out = out + c` or `out = out + escaped_char` with a list accumulator that collects characters and joins the list into a single string at the end, eliminating the O(n²) cost of incremental string building. The same approach should be applied to the JSON‑document builder to reuse list appends and direct assignments instead of recreating collections on each step.
