In the `_parse_string` routine, replace the repeated `out = out + c` assignments with a character‑accumulation list that is filled with `out.append(c)` during the parse and joined into a single string only after the loop finishes. This eliminates the O(n²) cost of repeated string concatenation while preserving all special‑escape and control‑character handling. The resulting string is produced once via `''.join(chars)`, giving a linear‑time operation.
