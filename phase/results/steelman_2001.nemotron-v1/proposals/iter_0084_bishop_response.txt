Replace the repeated string concatenation within `_parse_string` with a list buffer that collects characters during parsing, then join the list into a single string at the end of the function; this eliminates the O(n²) cost of building strings step‑by‑step and avoids the overhead of many small string allocations. The change is limited to the body of `_parse_string` where `out = out + c` is used, replacing each concatenation with a list `chars.append(c)` and a final `return ''.join(chars)`.
