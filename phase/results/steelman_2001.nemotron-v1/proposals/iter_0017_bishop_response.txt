Replace the repeated string concatenations such as `out = out + "\f"` and `out = out + c` with in‑memory appends to a list (`out.append("\f")` or `out.append(c)`) and, once the parsing step is complete, join the list into a single string with `str.join`. This eliminates the O(k²) cost of building strings via concatenation, reducing the time spent inside the loop.
