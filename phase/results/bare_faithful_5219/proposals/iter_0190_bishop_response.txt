To optimize the JSON parser, we can avoid scanning numbers character-by-character and instead scan them in larger chunks. This approach allows us to skip over large sections of text that don't contain numbers, thus reducing the number of iterations required to parse the input. The specific change is to maintain a state variable `_num_start` that tracks the position where the start of a number was found. When we encounter a digit, we can scan the rest of the string starting from this position, rather than scanning each character individually. This reduces the time complexity of parsing numbers and improves the overall performance of the parser.
