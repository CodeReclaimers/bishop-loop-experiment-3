CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while-loop in `_skip_ws` with a regex would be unworkable because regex engines are not optimized for simple character skipping and would introduce significant overhead. It would also break the incremental parsing model, as regex matching typically doesn't support resumable state across multiple calls. Additionally, the claimed O(1) complexity is incorrect — regex matching still needs to scan input, just with different algorithmic characteristics.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use a compiled regex to skip whitespace in one go, but only when there's a significant amount of whitespace to skip, and to integrate this into the existing parsing loop to avoid repeated function calls. This preserves the performance gains while maintaining correctness and avoiding the overhead of full regex engine initialization per call.
