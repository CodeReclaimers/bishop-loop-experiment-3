Generator expressions would be irrelevant here since the issue isn't about building collections but about the recursive descent structure and redundant whitespace handling.
STEELMAN: A better approach would be to eliminate the redundant whitespace handling at every level of recursion by pre-processing the input to remove all whitespace, or at least reduce the number of `_skip_ws` calls by batching them, thereby reducing the overhead of repeated index checks and function calls during parsing.
