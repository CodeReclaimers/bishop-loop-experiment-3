In `json_parser.py`, consider replacing the use of explicit predicates in `_parse_value` with a more efficient string scanning mechanism that directly parses numbers character-by-character without using conditional statements for each digit and decimal point. This could be achieved by maintaining a running total of the number being parsed, allowing characters to be added or subtracted as they are encountered. This approach can reduce the time complexity from O(n) to O(1), where n is the length of the input string.
