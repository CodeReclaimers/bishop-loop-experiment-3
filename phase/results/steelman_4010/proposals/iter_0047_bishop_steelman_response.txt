Additionally, it fails to address the core performance bottleneck which is the repeated `_skip_ws` calls during recursive parsing, not just at the start. The approach also doesn't consider that the input might be a very large string where even a single `strip()` call could be expensive.

This preserves the correctness of the parser while reducing redundant calls to `_skip_ws` at the top level.
