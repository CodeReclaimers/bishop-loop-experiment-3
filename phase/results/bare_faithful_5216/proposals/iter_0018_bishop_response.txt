To optimize the JSON parser, we can make use of a stack-based approach to avoid recursion. This will reduce the overhead of function calls and improve performance. The idea is to maintain a stack that keeps track of pending elements during parsing. When an opening brace `{` or `[` is encountered, it is pushed onto the stack. When a closing brace `}` or `]` is encountered, it is popped from the stack. This ensures that each pair of braces is properly matched and closed in the correct order.
