Replace the `_WS_RE = re.compile(r'\s*')` and all uses of regex‑based whitespace skipping with a call to `text.find('\S', pos)` to locate the next non‑whitespace character directly, thereby avoiding the overhead of a compiled regular‑expression match. This changes both the outer `parse` loop and the `_parse_value` loop, which are the hot paths that repeatedly advance over whitespace.
