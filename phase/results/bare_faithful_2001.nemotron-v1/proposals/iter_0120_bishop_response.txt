Replace the use of the compiled regex _WS_RE for skipping whitespace by a simple character‑by‑character loop that advances pos while text[pos].isspace() is true; this eliminates the overhead of a regular expression engine and makes the whitespace‑skipping step O(n) with minimal code. The loop can be inserted at the start of parse and _parse_value instead of using _WS_RE.match or find. This change removes the regex allocation and parsing cost while preserving correctness.
