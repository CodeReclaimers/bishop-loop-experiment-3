Replace the repeated use of `str.find` or the regular‑expression‑based `_skip_ws` function with a constant‑time lookup using a pre‑computed byte‑table that maps each character’s ASCII value to whether it is whitespace. This eliminates the loop‑over‑whitespace iteration and reduces per‑character overhead, while still correctly advancing the index over any sequence of spaces, tabs, newlines, or carriage returns.
