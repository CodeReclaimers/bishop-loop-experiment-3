In _parse_value, replace the manual whitespace‑skipping while loop with a single call to _WS_RE.finditer to jump to the next non‑whitespace character, eliminating the repeated per‑character checks. This reduces the overhead of evaluating each character in ‘\x20\t\n\r’ on every token, making the parser faster while preserving correctness. The same pattern can be applied to the other parsing functions that currently scan whitespace incrementally.
