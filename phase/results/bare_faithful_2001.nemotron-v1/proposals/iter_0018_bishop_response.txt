Replace the per‑call call to `_skip_ws` that uses a regular expression with a lightweight loop that simply advances `pos` while the current character is one of `' \t\n\r'`. This avoids the overhead of regex compilation and matching, letting the parser skip whitespace in O(1) per character instead of O(m) where `m` is the match length. Pre‑compute the set of whitespace characters once at module load to further reduce overhead.
