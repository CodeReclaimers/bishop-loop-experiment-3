Replace the `_skip_ws` function’s regex‑based whitespace skipping with a direct loop that advances `pos` while `ord(text[pos])` is one of `0x20, 0x09, 0x0A, 0x0D`, and return the final `pos` value. This eliminates the overhead of a compiled regular expression and the per‑call iterator, making the whitespace‑skipping step O(n) with fewer function calls.
