Replace every occurrence of `pos = text.find('\S', pos)` in the whitespace‑skipping sections with a manual loop that increments `pos` while it lands on a whitespace (`while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in ' \t\n\r': pos += 1`). This removes the call‑overhead of repeatedly searching the string and applies to all `_parse_*` functions that currently skip whitespace. The gain comes from avoiding the function call and regex‑like work of `find` each time.
