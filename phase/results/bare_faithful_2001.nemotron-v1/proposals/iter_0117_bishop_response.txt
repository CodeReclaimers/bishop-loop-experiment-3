Replace the manual position‑advancing loops that scan whitespace character‑by‑character with a single call to the pre‑compiled `\s*` regex (e.g., `pos = _WS_RE.search(text, pos).end()`) to jump directly to the next non‑whitespace character, which is faster because the regex engine does the scanning in compiled C code. This change applies to both `parse` and `_parse_object` where whitespace is skipped.
