Replace the per‑character whitespace‑skipping loops in the parser (both in `parse`, `_parse_value`, and `_parse_object`) with calls to `text.find('\S', pos)` to jump directly to the next non‑whitespace character, eliminating the O(n) scan of each character. This eliminates the repeated `while pos < len(text): if text[pos] not in ' \t\n\r': pos += 1` checks and reduces overhead without affecting correctness.
