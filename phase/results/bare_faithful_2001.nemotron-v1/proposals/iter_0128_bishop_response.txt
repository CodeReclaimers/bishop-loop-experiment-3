Replace the regex‑based whitespace skips (the _WS_RE = re.compile(r'\s*') call and the re.sub(r'\s+', '', text) pre‑processing) with a simple incremental scan that uses text.find('\S', pos) to locate the next non‑whitespace character, scanning forward one character at a time instead of invoking the regex engine. This removes the overhead of regular‑expression compilation and string mutation while preserving the same logic for advancing the parser position.
