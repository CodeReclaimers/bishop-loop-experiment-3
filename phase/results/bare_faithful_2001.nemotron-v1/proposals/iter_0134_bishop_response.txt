Replace the repeated whitespace‑skipping that calls `WS_RE.search` with a direct `text.find('\S', pos)` call inside `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_array`, and `_parse_string`; this eliminates the overhead of preparing and scanning a compiled regex each time. By doing so, the parser uses a simple linear‑time string scan that is faster than the regex‑based approach while preserving the same whitespace‑skipping behavior.
