Replace the repeated `re.match(r'\s*', text, pos)` calls that perform a full regex match each time whitespace is skipped with a single, pre‑compiled regex pattern (e.g., `WS_PATTERN = re.compile(r'\s')`) and use `WS_PATTERN.match(text, pos).end()` instead. This reduces the overhead of re‑creating a regex object and avoids the tiny cost of the `re.match` call, while still correctly advancing `pos` over any whitespace. The change is limited to the `_skip_ws` helper and its usage throughout the parser.
