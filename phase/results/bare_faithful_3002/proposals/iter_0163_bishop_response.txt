Replace the `_skip_ws` helper and the two `re.match` calls in `parse` with a simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in " \t\n\r": pos += 1` loop instead of using `re.match`. This eliminates the overhead of the regular‑expression engine and the temporary match object, allowing whitespace to be skipped in‑place with a tight, low‑level Python loop. The change is safe because the loop performs exactly the same character‑by‑character check that the original regex was doing.
