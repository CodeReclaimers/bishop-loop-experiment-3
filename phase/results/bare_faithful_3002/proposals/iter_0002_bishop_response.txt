Replace the repeated calls to `re.sub` in `_skip_ws` with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos] in " \t\n\r"` instead of invoking the regex each time. This eliminates the overhead of creating and matching a regular expression on every whitespace skip, making the function call faster while preserving the exact same whitespace‑skipping behavior. This change targets only `_skip_ws` and its usage in `parse`.
