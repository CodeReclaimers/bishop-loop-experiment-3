Replace the manual whitespace‑skipping loop in both `_skip_ws` and the calls to it at the top of `_parse_value` with a single regex search that finds the next non‑whitespace character: `pos = re.search(r'\S', text[pos:]).start()` and then add one to it. This avoids iterating character‑by‑character and instead jumps directly to the next token, which is asymptotically faster while still correctly consuming the same whitespace sequence.
