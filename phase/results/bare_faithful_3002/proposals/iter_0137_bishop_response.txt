Replace the calls to `_skip_ws` with a simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in " \t\n\r": pos += 1` loop instead of using `re.match`. This eliminates the overhead of a regular‑expression match for each whitespace skip, which is the dominant cost, while preserving the exact same logic. The change is only in `parse`, `_parse_value`, and `_parse_object` where `_skip_ws` is invoked.
