Replace the manual, per‑character whitespace‑skipping loops in `parse`, `_skip_ws`, `_parse_value`, and other functions with a single call to a compiled regular expression that consumes all whitespace characters at once (e.g., `re.match(r'\s+', text, pos)`). This leverages the C‑level implementation of the regex engine, which is orders of magnitude faster than the Python‑level `while` loop that checks each character individually.
