Replace the two calls to `re.match(r'\s*', text, pos)` in `parse` and the `_skip_ws` function with a lightweight loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is one of `" \t\n\r"`. This avoids re‑compiling a regex and the cost of a match‑and‑end operation each time, letting the simple `while` check run in O(1) bytecode per character.
