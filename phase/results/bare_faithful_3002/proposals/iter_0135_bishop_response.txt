Replace the regex‑based whitespace‑skipping calls (the `re.match(r'\s*', …)` in `parse` and `_skip_ws`) with a simple while‑loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos]` is one of “ \t\n\r”. Remove the `_skip_ws` helper entirely and inline the loop so whitespace is handled by direct character comparisons, which is faster than invoking the regex engine. This reduces function call overhead and avoids creating match objects for each whitespace stretch.
