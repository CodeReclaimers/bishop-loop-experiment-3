Replace the repeated `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in " \t\n\r"` checks with a single `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_SET:` where `_WS_SET = frozenset(" \t\n\r")` is defined at the top, using O(1) set membership instead of O(4) string scans. This eliminates the small constant overhead per whitespace character and speeds up whitespace skipping throughout the parser.
