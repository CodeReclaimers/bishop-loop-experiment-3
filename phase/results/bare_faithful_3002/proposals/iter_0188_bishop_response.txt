Replace the regex‑based whitespace skipping in both `_skip_ws` and the whitespace‑skip loops inside the parser with simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in " \t\n\r": pos += 1` loops; this eliminates the regex engine call and lets the interpreter use a fast, low‑overhead character check. The change keeps correctness because the same whitespace is still consumed linearly and the same characters are compared.
