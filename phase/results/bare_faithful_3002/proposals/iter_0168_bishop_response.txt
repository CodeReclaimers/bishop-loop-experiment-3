Replace the manual ord‑based whitespace skips (`ord(text[pos]) < 32`) with a direct membership test against the literal `" \t\n\r"` (or, even faster, a compiled regex `\s*`) in the `_skip_ws` and `parse` functions. This removes costly ord calls and leverages fast C‑level string matching, which is significantly quicker while preserving the same whitespace‑skipping behavior.
