Replace the global `_WS_RE` regex and its usage in `_skip_ws` with a direct call to `text.find('\S', pos)`, and remove the `re` import and `_WS_RE` variable entirely. This eliminates the cost of compiling and matching a regular expression for a simple whitespace‑skip operation, which is the dominant overhead in the parser.
