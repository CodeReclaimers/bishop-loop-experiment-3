In _parse_number, replace the intermediate string variable s = text[start:pos] with a direct call to int(text[start:pos]) (and similarly float(text[start:pos])) so the conversion to int or float works on the slice itself rather than creating a temporary string object. This eliminates the allocation of an extra string, letting Python’s built‑in int/float constructors operate on the same slice and thus reducing overhead without changing the parsed result.
