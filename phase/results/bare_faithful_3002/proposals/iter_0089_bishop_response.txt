Replace the `_skip_ws` implementation that relies on the compiled `_WS_RE` regex with a direct loop that checks `text[pos]` against a constant whitespace set (`" \t\n\r"`), and remove the global `_WS_RE` so the parser avoids the overhead of calling `re.sub` each time.
