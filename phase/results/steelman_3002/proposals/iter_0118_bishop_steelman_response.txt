Additionally, the regex would need to be recompiled or reset for each call, negating performance gains and potentially causing incorrect behavior when whitespace spans across multiple calls.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use a precompiled regex with `re.match()` or `re.search()` that matches zero or more whitespace characters starting from `pos`, and then advance the position to the end of that match.
