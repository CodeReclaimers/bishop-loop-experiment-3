Replace the frozenset membership checks for digits (e.g., `c in _DIGIT_CHARS`) with direct ASCII range comparisons (`c >= '0' and c <= '9'`), which avoid the overhead of constructing a frozenset lookup and are faster. This change should be made in `_parse_value` (both for key parsing and the number parsing loop) and in `_parse_number` where digits are processed. The direct range test is equivalent to the frozenset check and keeps the parser correct.
