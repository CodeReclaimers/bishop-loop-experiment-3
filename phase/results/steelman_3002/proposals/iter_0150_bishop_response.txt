Replace the call to `_WS_RE` in the `parse` function with a direct call to `text.find(' \t\n\r', pos)` to skip whitespace entirely. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and executing a regular expression for each whitespace skip, which can be a bottleneck. The same `find` logic is already present in the `_skip_ws` implementations, so using it directly speeds up the parsing while preserving correctness.
