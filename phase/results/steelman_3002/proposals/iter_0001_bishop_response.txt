One way to speed up `_parse_object` is to replace the repeated `out = dict(out)` and `out[key] = value` assignments with a list that collects the key‑value pairs, and then build the final dictionary once at the end, eliminating the O(k) dict rebuilds per iteration. Similarly, for `_parse_array` we can keep the list growing with `out.append(value)` instead of rebuilding it each step.
