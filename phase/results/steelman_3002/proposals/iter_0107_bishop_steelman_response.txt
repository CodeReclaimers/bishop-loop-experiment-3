Additionally, `str.find` would not handle the case where the string ends without whitespace, potentially causing incorrect indexing or errors.

STEELMAN: The steelman version would use `str.find` to locate the next non-whitespace character by searching for any character not in the whitespace set, and jump directly to that position.
