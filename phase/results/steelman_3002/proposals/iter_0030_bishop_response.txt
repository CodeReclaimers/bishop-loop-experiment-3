Replace the character‑by‑character checks that advance `pos` one position at a time within `_parse_value` and its sub‑parsers with a single `str.find` call that directly locates the next token (e.g., `{`, `[`, `}`, `]`, `:` or `,`) and sets `pos` to that index, so the parser skips ahead in O(1) work instead of O(n) string scanning.
