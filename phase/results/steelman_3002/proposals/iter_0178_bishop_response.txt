Replace the `_skip_ws` function’s inner loop with a single call to `text.find(' \t\n\r', pos)` that returns the index of the next non‑whitespace character, instead of using the pre‑compiled regex `_WS_RE`. This removes the overhead of parsing and matching the regex pattern on every call, turning whitespace skipping into an O(1) linear‑scan operation. The rest of the parser can stay unchanged, and correctness is preserved because the same characters are skipped.
