Replace the tight character‑by‑character `_skip_ws` loop that calls the pre‑compiled regex `_WS_RE` with a single `str.find` call that locates the next non‑whitespace character, advancing `pos` directly, which eliminates repeated regex invocations and the inner loop, yielding a much smaller per‑character overhead. This change keeps the correct whitespace handling while cutting the dominant cost of the parser.
