In _parse_value and _parse_number replace the set‑membership checks (e.g., c in _DIGIT_CHARS, c in _HEX_CHARS) with direct ord‑based comparisons that avoid the cost of hashing or set lookups. This removes the per‑character overhead of set look‑ups and makes the digit and hexadecimal parsing branch faster while preserving correctness. The change should be applied only to those membership tests, leaving the rest of the logic unchanged.
