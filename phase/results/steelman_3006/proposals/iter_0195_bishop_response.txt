Replace the while‑loop in _skip_ws that checks each character against the set _WS_CHARS with a direct bit‑mask test that advances the index as long as ord(text[pos]) has the space (0x20) or tab (0x09) high‑bit cleared, i.e., (ord(text[pos]) & 0x20 == 0) and (ord(text[pos]) & 0x10 == 0). This C‑level bitwise operation avoids the set‑membership lookup and is faster while preserving the same whitespace definition.
