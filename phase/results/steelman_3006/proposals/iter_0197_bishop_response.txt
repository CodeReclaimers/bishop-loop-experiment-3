Replace the set‑membership checks `_WS_CHARS`, `_DIGIT_CHARS`, `_HEX_CHARS`, and the similar range checks in `_parse_number` with direct `ord` comparisons (e.g., `ord(c) >= 48 and ord(c) <= 57` for digits, `ord(c) >= 97 and ord(c) <= 102` or `ord(c) >= 65 and ord(c) <= 70` for hex) to eliminate the O(1) hash table lookups.
