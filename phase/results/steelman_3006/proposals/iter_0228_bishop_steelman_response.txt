CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `text.translate` with a translation table is unworkable because `translate` removes characters rather than skipping them, which would fundamentally alter the parsing logic by destroying the input string. It also doesn't preserve the position tracking needed for the parser to know where it left off after skipping whitespace.
