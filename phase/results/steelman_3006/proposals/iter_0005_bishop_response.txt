In the public `parse` function, retrieve the length of the input string once and store it in a local variable `n = len(text)` before any positional checks.  
Then replace every occurrence of `len(text)` with `n` (e.g., in `_skip_ws`, `_parse_value`, and the trailing‑content assertion).  
Caching the length avoids repeated function calls and attribute lookups, which reduces overhead and speeds up the parser without altering its logic.
