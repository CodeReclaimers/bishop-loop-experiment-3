Remove the `_is_ws_char`, `_is_digit_char` and `_is_hex_char` helper functions and inline their bitwise‑mask logic directly inside `_skip_ws`, `_parse_number`, `_parse_string`, and the character‑recognition checks, so each character test is performed with a single O(1) bitwise operation instead of a function call.
