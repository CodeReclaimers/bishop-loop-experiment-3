CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the character-by-character loop with a single `text.find` call is flawed because `text.find` would only locate the first non-whitespace character, not skip an entire run of whitespace. This approach fails to handle consecutive whitespace characters efficiently and would still require multiple calls to skip whitespace, negating the intended performance benefit. Additionally, `text.find` is not necessarily O(1) in practice, as it still needs to scan through characters until a match is found.
