Replace the _is_ws_char, _is_digit_char, and _is_hex_char functions with inline bit‑wise tests that use a single pre‑computed lookup table, so _skip_ws and the parsing loops can check membership with a single bitwise operation instead of a function call. This removes the overhead of calling those helper functions and the extra bytecode indirection, making the whitespace‑skipping and character‑type checks significantly faster.
