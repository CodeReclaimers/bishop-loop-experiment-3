Replace the incremental character‑by‑character loop in `_skip_ws` with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` to locate the next non‑whitespace character in one operation, falling back to the loop only when the result is `-1` or when the next character is not a whitespace. This reduces the number of per‑character checks from O(n) to O(1) for long whitespace runs.
