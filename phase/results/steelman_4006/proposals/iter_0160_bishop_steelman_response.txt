This approach would break the existing design that relies heavily on substring operations and forward-looking character checks, especially in functions like `_parse_number` and `_parse_string` where lookahead is essential. Additionally, the proposed method would likely introduce more overhead due to function call overhead from iteration, negating any gains from skipping whitespace.
