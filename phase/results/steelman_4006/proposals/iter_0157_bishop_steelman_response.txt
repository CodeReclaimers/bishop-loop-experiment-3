The approach would also break the clean separation of escape sequences and their interpretations.

STEELMAN: A better approach would be to precompute a lookup table for common escape sequences (like \n, \t, etc.) using a list of tuples, but keep the Unicode escape handling separate with a dedicated function or optimized path.
