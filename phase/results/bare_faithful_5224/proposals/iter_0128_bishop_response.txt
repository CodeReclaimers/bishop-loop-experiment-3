To optimize the `parse` function, we can use an iterative approach to parse JSON objects and arrays instead of using recursion. This will reduce the overhead of recursive calls and improve performance. We can achieve this by maintaining a stack of elements during parsing and pushing new elements onto the stack when encountering values. When we encounter a closing brace or square bracket, we pop elements from the stack until we reach the corresponding opening brace or square bracket. This approach avoids the overhead of recursion and allows us to parse JSON structures more efficiently.
