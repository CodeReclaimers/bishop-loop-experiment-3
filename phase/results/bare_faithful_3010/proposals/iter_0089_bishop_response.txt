Replace the step‑by‑step whitespace checks that increment `pos` by one with a single call to the pre‑compiled `_WS_PATTERN` regex (e.g., using `pos = _WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(text, pos).end()`) in both `_parse_value` and `_parse_object`, allowing the parser to skip any amount of whitespace in one operation instead of looping character by character.
