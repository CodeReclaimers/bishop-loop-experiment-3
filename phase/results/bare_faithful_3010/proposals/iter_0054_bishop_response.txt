Replace the whitespace‑skipping step inside `_parse_object` (lines 70‑80) with a direct loop `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` instead of using `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(text, pos).end()`.
