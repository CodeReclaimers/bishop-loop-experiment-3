Replace the manual character‑by‑character whitespace loops in `parse`, `_parse_value`, `_parse_string`, and `_parse_boolean` with a single call to the pre‑compiled `_WS_PATTERN.search()` to obtain the next non‑whitespace position, then set `pos` to that match’s end. This eliminates repeated `while` iterations and leverages the highly optimized C implementation of the regular expression, yielding the same correctness while cutting the overhead to O(1) per whitespace block.
