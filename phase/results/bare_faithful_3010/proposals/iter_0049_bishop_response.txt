Replace the per‑character while loops that skip whitespace (e.g., `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1`) inside functions such as `_parse_value` and `_parse_object` with a single call to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(text, pos).end()`, which jumps to the end of the whole whitespace block in one operation and eliminates the repeated Python‑level iteration overhead.
