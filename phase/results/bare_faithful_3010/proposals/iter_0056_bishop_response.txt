Replace the inner whitespace‑skipping loops (e.g., in `_parse_object`, `_parse_value`, `_parse_string`) that repeatedly call `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search` with a single call to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(...).end()` and then a simple loop that advances `pos` until the next non‑whitespace character; this avoids repeated regex‑match overhead while preserving the same O‑length behavior.
