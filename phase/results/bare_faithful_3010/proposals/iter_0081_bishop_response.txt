Replace the repeated while‑loops that increment pos one character at a time with calls to the compiled regex pattern `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(text, pos).end()` to skip over any amount of whitespace in one O(1) operation. This eliminates the O(n) cost of checking each character individually and leverages the already‑pre‑compiled regex for fast advancement. The change applies to all whitespace‑skipping steps in `parse`, `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, and `_skip_ws`.
