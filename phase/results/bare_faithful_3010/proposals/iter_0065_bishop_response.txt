Replace every call to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search` with a direct loop over `_WS_CHARS` using `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1`, so whitespace skipping no longer relies on regex compilation and is just a simple O(1) character‑by‑character check.
