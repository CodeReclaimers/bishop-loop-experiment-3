In the main loop of `parse` and the whitespace‑skipping sections inside `_parse_object` and `_parse_value`, replace the repeated `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loops with a single call to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(text, pos).end()`.
