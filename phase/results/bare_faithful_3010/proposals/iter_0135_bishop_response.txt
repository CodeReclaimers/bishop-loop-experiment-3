Replace the repeated `pos = _WS_PATTERN.pattern.span(pos)[1]` calls with a simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loop, which iteratively skips characters instead of invoking a regex engine each time. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and matching the pattern for every whitespace segment and is O(1) per character, yielding faster parsing while preserving correctness.
