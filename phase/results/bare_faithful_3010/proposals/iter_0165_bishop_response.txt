Replace the repetitive character‑by‑character checks that skip whitespace (e.g., `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1`) with a single call to `_WS_PATTERN.match(text, pos)` that returns the position after the whole whitespace run, advancing `pos` to `match.end()` instead of looping. This avoids the overhead of many Python bytecode instructions and keeps the same O(1)‑ish skip per token.
