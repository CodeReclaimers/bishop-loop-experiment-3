Replace the regex‑based whitespace‑skipping in the `parse` function and all calls to `_parse_object` with a simple loop that checks `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS` and increments `pos`; this eliminates the overhead of compiling and matching the regex each time and uses the pre‑computed frozenset for O(1) membership tests.
