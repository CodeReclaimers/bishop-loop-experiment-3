Replace the repeated regex‑based whitespace‑skipping calls (e.g., `_skip_ws` or `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search`) with simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loops, which avoid the overhead of compiling and executing a regular expression each time. This direct membership check is O(1) per character and eliminates the function call cost, making the parser significantly faster while preserving correctness.
