Replace the inner while‑loops that advance `pos` one character at a time by using the compiled regex `_WS_PATTERN` to jump directly to the next non‑whitespace character via `m.end()` (or `m.start()` for the last match) in a single operation. This eliminates the O(n) character‑by‑character checks in `parse` and `_parse_object` and reduces the per‑skip cost from linear scans to a constant‑time regex match.
