Replace the repeated regex look‑ups in the parsing loops (both `parse` and `_parse_object`/`_parse_array`) with a simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` check, which avoids the cost of compiling and matching the `_WS_PATTERN` each iteration. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching while still correctly skipping whitespace because the loop uses the precomputed frozenset `_WS_CHARS`.
