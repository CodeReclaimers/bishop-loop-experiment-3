Replace the manual character‑by‑character whitespace‑skipping loops in `_parse_object` and `_parse_value` with a single call to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(text, pos).end()`, letting the compiled regex jump directly to the next non‑whitespace position instead of advancing one character at a time.
