Replace all calls to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.search(...).end()` in both `parse` and `_parse_object` with a direct `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loop, so whitespace skipping is performed with a simple index increment instead of invoking the compiled regex each time. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching and keeps the O(1) character‑set checks, speeding up whitespace skipping.
