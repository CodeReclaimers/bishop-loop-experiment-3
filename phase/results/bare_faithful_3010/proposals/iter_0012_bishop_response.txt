Replace all Python‑level loops that skip whitespace (e.g., `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1`) with a single call to `text.find_first_not_of(' \t\n\r', pos)`, which is implemented in C and avoids the overhead of iterating manually and the compiled regex _WS_PATTERN.
