Replace the regex‑based `_skip_ws` call with a simple loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos]` is in the pre‑computed `_WS_CHARS` set, avoiding the cost of compiling and matching the pattern each time. Since `_WS_CHARS` is a frozenset, the membership test is O(1) and the loop runs in linear time without the overhead of a regex engine. This change speeds up whitespace skipping while preserving the same behavior.
