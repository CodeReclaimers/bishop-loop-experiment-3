Skip the regex call in both the main `parse` function and inside `_parse_object` and `_parse_array` by replacing `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.span(pos)[1]` with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is in `_WS_CHARS`. This removes the overhead of calling the compiled regex each time, turning O(1) per character into a direct O(k) scan that is faster for short whitespace runs.
