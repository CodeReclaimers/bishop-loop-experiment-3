Replace the repeated calls to `_WS_PATTERN.pattern.span(pos)[1]` with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is in the pre‑computed `_WS_CHARS` set. This removes the overhead of invoking the regex engine on every whitespace skip and keeps the same O(1) membership check. The change applies to both the top‑level `parse` and all helper functions (`_parse_object`, `_parse_array`, etc.).
