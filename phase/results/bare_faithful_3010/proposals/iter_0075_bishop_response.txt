Move the whitespace‑skipping logic out of the regex calls and replace them with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is in the pre‑computed `_WS_CHARS` set; this eliminates the overhead of recompiling and executing the pattern repeatedly. The same loop can be used in `_parse_object`, `_parse_value`, and `_parse_string` wherever whitespace is skipped, keeping the same correctness.
