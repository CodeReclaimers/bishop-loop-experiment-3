Replace the `_skip_ws` function and the regex `_WS_RE` with a simple loop that increments `pos` while reading whitespace characters, removing the per‑iteration regex call that currently drives most of the parsing overhead. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and matching the regex for every whitespace segment, while the same logic (skipping spaces, tabs, newlines, and carriage returns) is preserved.
