Replace the precompiled regular expression _WS_RE = re.compile(r'\s*') and all its calls (e.g., _skip_ws and the initial match in parse) with a simple while‑loop that advances the position past whitespace characters. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching and makes whitespace skipping O(1) per character. The rest of the parser can remain unchanged, preserving correctness while significantly speeding up parsing.
