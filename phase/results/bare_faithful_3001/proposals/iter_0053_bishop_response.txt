Replace the `_skip_ws` function’s regex call with a direct call to `text.find(" \t\n\r", pos)` to advance `pos` over whitespace in one step instead of using a compiled regex. This eliminates the overhead of matching a regular expression on each call and still correctly skips any combination of spaces, tabs, newlines, and carriage returns. The change is isolated to `_skip_ws` and its usage in `parse`, `_parse_value`, and related functions.
