Replace the `_skip_ws` function and all calls to `_WS_RE.match` with a simple while‑loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos]` is one of the whitespace characters, and remove the regex completely; this eliminates the overhead of compiling and invoking a regular‑expression match for each whitespace segment.
