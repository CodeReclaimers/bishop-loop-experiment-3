Replace the conversion to a ``bytearray`` and the subsequent byte‑level ``_skip_ws`` routine with direct work on the original ``str``; this lets the parser iterate the string with its native ``str[pos]`` and ``pos += 1`` operations, avoiding the overhead of creating a byte buffer and comparing byte values for whitespace.
