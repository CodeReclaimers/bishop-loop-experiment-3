Replace the regex‑based whitespace skipping (`_WS_RE.match` and `m.end()`) with a simple `while` loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos]` is a space, tab, newline, or carriage‑return. This avoids the overhead of compiling and matching a regular expression on every iteration. The loop already correctly skips whitespace, so removing the regex call keeps correctness while improving speed.
