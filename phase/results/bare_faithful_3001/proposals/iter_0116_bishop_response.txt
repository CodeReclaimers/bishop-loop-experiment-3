Replace the regex‑based whitespace skipping in the `parse` function and `_parse_value` helper with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while the current character is a space, tab, newline or carriage‑return, removing the need for `_skip_ws` and `_WS_RE`. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and matching a regular expression for every whitespace segment, which is the bottleneck, while keeping the same correctness logic.
