Replace the `_skip_ws` function calls in `parse`, `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, etc., with manual loops that advance `pos` while `text[pos] in " \t\n\r"` instead of using the `_WS_RE` compiled regular expression. This removes the overhead of compiling and searching the regex each time, turning whitespace skipping into a simple character‑by‑character advance that is faster while keeping the same behavior.
