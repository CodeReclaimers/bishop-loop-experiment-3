Replace the regular‑expression based whitespace skipping (`_WS_RE` and `_skip_ws`) with a simple Python loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is a space, tab, or newline. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and executing the regex each call, letting the interpreter use faster C‑level looping.
