Replace the repeated calls to `_skip_ws` that use the compiled `_WS_RE` regex with an inline `while` loop that increments `pos` until a non‑whitespace character is reached. Removing `_WS_RE` and the separate `_skip_ws` function eliminates the overhead of regex matching and function calls, making the whitespace‑skipping step O(k) where k is the number of skipped characters instead of O(n) per call. This change keeps the logic identical while providing a faster, single‑pass whitespace skip.
