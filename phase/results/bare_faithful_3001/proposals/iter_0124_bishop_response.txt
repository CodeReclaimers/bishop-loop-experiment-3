Replace the regular‑expression based whitespace skips in `_skip_ws` and the early‑on whitespace checks in `_parse_value` with a simple Python `while` loop that advances `pos` past each whitespace character without invoking the regex. This eliminates the costly `re.match` call per character and reduces the overhead of regex compilation and pattern matching, while still correctly skipping spaces, tabs, newlines, and carriage returns.
