Replace the regular‑expression based `_skip_ws` with a simple while‑loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos].isspace()` is true, removing the `re` import and the compiled pattern. This avoids the overhead of compiling and matching on a substring, letting the parser skip whitespace by directly advancing the index. Since the loop is O(k) for k whitespace characters and the regex version also has overhead, the overall parsing time improves.
