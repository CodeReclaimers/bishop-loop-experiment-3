Replace the regex‑based `_skip_ws` function with a straightforward forward scan that advances `pos` past any whitespace characters without invoking the compiled pattern. This eliminates the overhead of a regular‑expression search on each call. By using a simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos].isspace(): pos += 1` loop, whitespace skipping becomes O(k) where k is the number of whitespace characters, which is faster than the regex engine’s overhead.
