Replace the `_skip_ws` helper with an inline `while pos < len(text) and text[pos].isspace(): pos += 1` loop inside each parser so whitespace is skipped directly without a function call. This eliminates the overhead of invoking `_skip_ws` repeatedly and keeps the same correct behavior. The change is limited to the `parse`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_array`, `_parse_string`, `_parse_boolean`, `_parse_null`, and `_parse_number` functions.
