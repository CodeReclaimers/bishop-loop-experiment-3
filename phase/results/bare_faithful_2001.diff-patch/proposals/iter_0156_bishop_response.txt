Replace the `_skip_ws` function with a manual loop that skips whitespace characters directly by advancing the index while `text[pos]` is a space or tab, rather than using `_WS_RE.match`. This eliminates the overhead of a regular‑expression match on every call, making the parser faster. The change only affects the whitespace‑skipping step in `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, and `_parse_array`.
