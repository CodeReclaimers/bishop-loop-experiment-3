Replace the `_skip_ws` function’s regular‑expression call with a tight Python loop that advances `pos` while `pos < len(text)` and `ord(text[pos]) > 32 and text[pos] not in "\r\n\t\f\v"`; this avoids the overhead of regex matching for every whitespace skip.  
Since all JSON whitespace is a subset of Unicode characters with `ord > 32` except line‑breaks, the loop is both correct and much faster.
