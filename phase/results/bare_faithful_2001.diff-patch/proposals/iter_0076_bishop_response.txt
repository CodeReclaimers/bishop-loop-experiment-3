Replace the `_skip_ws` helper with a simple `while` loop that advances `pos` as long as `text[pos]` is one of the whitespace characters (`' \t\n\r\f\v'`), avoiding the overhead of calling a compiled regular‑expression match each time. This eliminates the regex compilation and match overhead for every whitespace skip, which is the dominant cost in parsing. The rest of the parser can stay unchanged, so correctness is preserved.
