Replace the `_skip_ws` function with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is one of the whitespace characters ‘ ’, ‘\t’, ‘\n’, ‘\r’, ‘\f’, or ‘\v’. This eliminates the overhead of calling the compiled regular‑expression `_WS_RE.match` on every call, reducing the per‑character cost to a few byte‑code operations while preserving the same behavior.
