Replace the `_skip_ws` function with a simple loop that skips whitespace characters directly from the current position, instead of invoking the compiled regex `_WS_RE.match`. This eliminates the overhead of regex compilation and matching for each whitespace skip. The loop advances the index past any ‘‘0‑9’’, ‘ ‘, ‘\t’, ‘\n’, ‘\r’, ‘\f’, and ‘\v’ characters, which is faster for the repetitive whitespace removal performed throughout the parser.
