Replace the `_skip_ws` function with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while the character at that index is a whitespace character (space, tab, newline, carriage‑return, form‑feed, or vertical tab), instead of invoking the pre‑compiled `_WS_RE` regex. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching for every whitespace skip and makes the character‑level check a fast, bounded loop that still correctly skips any whitespace.
