Replace the manual character‑by‑character scans for booleans and numbers with fast `str.find` look‑ups that locate the end of the literal in one call; this eliminates repeated looping over the same characters. In `_parse_bool` use `pos = text.find("true", pos)` (or `"false"`) and in `_parse_number` use `pos = text.find(".", pos)` and `pos = text.find("e", pos)` (or `text.find("E", pos)`) to jump directly to the next non‑digit, then convert the slice with the built‑in `int`/`float` functions.
