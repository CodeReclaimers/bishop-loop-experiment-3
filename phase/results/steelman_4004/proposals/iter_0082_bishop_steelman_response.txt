CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while loop with `str.strip()` is fundamentally flawed because `strip()` removes ALL leading and trailing whitespace, not just leading whitespace, and returns the stripped string rather than the new position index. This would cause incorrect parsing behavior and fail to advance the parser position correctly. Additionally, `strip()` is not faster than a simple while loop for this use case and could actually be slower due to unnecessary operations on the entire remaining string.

STEELMAN: A better approach would be to use `str.lstrip()` to remove only leading whitespace, but still return the correct new position by calculating the difference between the original and stripped strings. However, even better would be to keep the while loop but optimize it with a more efficient approach like using `str.find()` or a regex for large runs of whitespace, while maintaining the position tracking semantics.
