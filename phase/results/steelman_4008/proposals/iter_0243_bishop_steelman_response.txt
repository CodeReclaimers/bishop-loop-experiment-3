CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.find` for large blocks of whitespace is problematic because `str.find` scans linearly and doesn't handle the case where whitespace is interleaved with non-whitespace efficiently. It also doesn't address the core issue that the current implementation already avoids regex, so the performance gain from `str.find` would be minimal compared to direct character scanning. Additionally, it doesn't consider that the typical case is small skips, making the large-block optimization irrelevant in most scenarios.

This preserves the core idea of avoiding regex while leveraging the efficiency of `str.find` for larger skips.
