CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.find` for whitespace skipping is problematic because `str.find` scans linearly and doesn't handle the case where whitespace is interleaved with non-whitespace efficiently. It also fails to account for the fact that whitespace skipping is a hot path and needs to be fast even for small inputs. Additionally, it doesn't address the overhead of repeatedly calling `str.find` on short segments where character-by-character scanning is already optimal.

STEELMAN: The steelman version uses a hybrid approach where `str.find` is used to skip large contiguous whitespace segments, but falls back to a character-by-character loop for small or edge cases. This balances performance for bulk whitespace while maintaining efficiency for short skips and avoiding the overhead of `str.find` on tiny inputs.
