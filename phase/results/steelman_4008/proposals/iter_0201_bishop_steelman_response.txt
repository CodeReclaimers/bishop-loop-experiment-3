CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.find` for whitespace skipping is problematic because `str.find` has O(n) complexity when searching for multiple characters, which defeats the purpose of optimization. It also fails to handle the case where whitespace is interleaved with other characters efficiently, and could introduce performance regressions in cases where whitespace is sparse or short.
STEELMAN: A better approach is to use a hybrid method: for large contiguous blocks of whitespace, use `str.find` to skip ahead quickly, but fall back to direct character scanning for smaller blocks or when the next non-whitespace character is close by. This maintains the performance benefits of bulk skipping while preserving correctness and avoiding the overhead of repeated `str.find` calls.
