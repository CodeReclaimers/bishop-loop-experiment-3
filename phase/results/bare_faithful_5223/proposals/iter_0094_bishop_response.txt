To optimize the JSON parser, we can consider using regular expressions instead of a custom whitespace skipping function. Regular expressions are more efficient for pattern matching in Python due to their built-in optimizations. This will allow us to skip white spaces faster and potentially speed up other parts of the parsing process as well. We can use `re.match()` or `re.finditer()` for scanning numbers, strings, and boolean values, which are optimized for regular expressions. This change should help in improving the overall performance of the parser without significantly affecting its correctness.
