One specific change to try is to use a more efficient way to skip whitespace in `_skip_ws` function. Instead of using `len(text[pos:].lstrip(_WS_CHARS))`, which scans through the entire string before skipping, we could implement a loop that skips the necessary amount of whitespace directly by moving the pointer forward until it encounters a non-whitespace character. This approach would reduce the time complexity from O(n) to O(m), where n is the length of the text and m is the number of characters in the skip set.
