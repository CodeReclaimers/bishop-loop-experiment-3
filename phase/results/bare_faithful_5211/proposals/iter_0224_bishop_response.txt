One improvement that could be made is to use a regular expression for whitespace skipping, instead of a custom function `_skip_ws`. Regular expressions can perform many operations in constant time, which would make the parsing faster. We could replace the current `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS:` with a single call to `re.match(r'\s+', text, pos)`, where `pos` is the current position in the text. This would allow us to skip all whitespace characters at once, without having to loop through each character individually.
