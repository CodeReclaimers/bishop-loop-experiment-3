CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the inline whitespace loop with a compiled regex is problematic because regex matching, while fast, introduces overhead from regex engine setup and backtracking that can be slower than simple iteration for small inputs. The claim of reducing time complexity from O(n) to O(1) is also misleading, as the regex still needs to scan the input.
