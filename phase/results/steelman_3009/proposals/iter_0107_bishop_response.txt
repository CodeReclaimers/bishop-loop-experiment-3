Replace the repeated whitespace‑skipping calls in `parse` and `_parse_value` (and their callees) with direct look‑ups into a pre‑computed `next_non_ws` array that gives the index of the next non‑whitespace character, eliminating the per‑character loop in `_skip_ws`.
