CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.translate` followed by `str.find` won't work because `str.translate` is designed to map characters to their replacements, not to remove them entirely. Simply translating whitespace characters to `None` or empty strings won't achieve the desired behavior of skipping whitespace. Additionally, `str.find` would not be suitable for finding the first non-whitespace character after translation, as it searches for a substring rather than performing the skip operation correctly.
