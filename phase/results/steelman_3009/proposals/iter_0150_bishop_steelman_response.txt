CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.translate` is unworkable because it would skip all whitespace characters in one go, but the parser needs to advance the position pointer to the first non-whitespace character, not just skip over all of it. Additionally, `str.translate` is not suitable for this use case since it's designed for character-to-character substitution, not position-based skipping.
STEELMAN: The steelman is to precompute a translation table that maps all whitespace characters to a single sentinel character (like `'\0'`), then use `str.translate` to replace all whitespace with that sentinel, followed by a `str.find` or `str.index` call to locate the first non-sentinel character.
