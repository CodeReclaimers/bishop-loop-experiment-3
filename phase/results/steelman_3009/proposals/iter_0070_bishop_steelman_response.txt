CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `re.compile(r'\s*')` would not work correctly because `\s*` matches zero or more whitespace characters but doesn't advance the position correctly in the context of a loop that needs to skip all consecutive whitespace. Additionally, the regex engine's performance benefit would be negated by the overhead of repeatedly calling `match()` on the same text from the same position, which is inefficient compared to a simple loop.
