Additionally, the "constant time" claim is misleading since the table lookup itself still requires O(n) preprocessing, and the approach doesn't scale well for large inputs or repeated calls. The idea of a "per-call" table is also problematic because it doesn't actually achieve constant-time behavior in practice.
