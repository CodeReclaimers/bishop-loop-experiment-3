Replace the manual character‑by‑character whitespace skip in _skip_ws with a single call to the pre‑compiled _WS_PATTERN search (e.g., pos = next(pos, WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos).start())) so the parser can jump past all whitespace in one step instead of looping over each character. This leverages the regex engine’s fast bulk‑skip operation while preserving the same start‑position semantics.
