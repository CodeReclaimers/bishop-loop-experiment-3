Additionally, `str.find` would need to be called repeatedly or in a loop to skip all consecutive whitespace, negating the benefit of avoiding per-character checks. The approach also fails to consider that `str.find` is not optimized for character-set lookups and would likely be slower than the current O(1) membership testing.
