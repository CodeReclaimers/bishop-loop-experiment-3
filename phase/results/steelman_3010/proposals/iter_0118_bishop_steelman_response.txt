CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the manual whitespace loop with `regex.search()` would be inefficient because regex engines are not optimized for simple character skipping; they're designed for complex pattern matching. The regex would still need to scan character-by-character to find the first non-whitespace, negating performance gains. Additionally, regex compilation overhead and backtracking could slow down the common case of minimal whitespace.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use the precompiled regex to skip whitespace, but only when there's a significant amount of whitespace to skip, and fall back to the fast character-set lookup for small amounts of whitespace. This maintains performance for both cases while leveraging the regex engine's bulk-matching capabilities.
