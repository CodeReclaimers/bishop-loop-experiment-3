Replace the per‑character whitespace‑skipping loops (e.g., in _skip_ws and every call to it) with a single call to the compiled regex _WS_PATTERN.sub, which consumes all consecutive whitespace characters in one pass. This eliminates the overhead of the while‑loop and branch‑predictions, especially in tight inner‑loop code such as parsing object keys and values.
