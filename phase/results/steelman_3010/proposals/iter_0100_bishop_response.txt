Replace the manual `_skip_ws` loop with a single call to the compiled regex `_WS_PATTERN` that matches and jumps over any amount of whitespace, so `pos = _WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos).start()` (or `pos = _WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos).end()`) skips whitespace in O(1) time instead of stepping through each character. This eliminates the per‑character membership checks and reduces function call overhead, making whitespace skipping faster while preserving correctness.
