CRITIQUE: The suggestion fails to account for the fact that JSON whitespace is not just simple space characters—it includes tabs, newlines, and carriage returns, and must be handled precisely at each parsing boundary. Replacing all whitespace with a single character would break the parser's ability to correctly identify where tokens begin and end, especially in cases like `{"key": "value"}` where the parser must distinguish between whitespace and actual structural delimiters. Additionally, the approach doesn't consider that the parser needs to maintain position tracking for error reporting, which would become ambiguous if all whitespace is collapsed.
