CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.translate` for whitespace skipping is flawed because `str.translate` maps characters to replacements, not to skip them, so it wouldn't naturally produce a count of whitespace characters. Additionally, `str.translate` is not necessarily faster than a simple loop for small amounts of whitespace, and the approach doesn't account for the fact that we need to advance the position by the length of the whitespace, not just find its presence.

STEELMAN: A better approach would be to use `str.rstrip` with a set of whitespace characters, or more accurately, to leverage a regex that matches a contiguous sequence of whitespace characters, which can be efficiently matched and whose length can be directly used to advance the position. This preserves the intent of reducing per-character overhead while maintaining correctness.
