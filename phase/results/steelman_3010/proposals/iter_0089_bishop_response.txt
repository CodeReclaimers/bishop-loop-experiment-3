Precompute a single skip‑table pass over the input string to produce a list where each entry stores the index of the next non‑whitespace character, then replace the manual `_skip_ws` loop with a direct lookup. This eliminates the per‑character `in _WS_CHARS` checks and the string‑building overhead, reducing the time spent skipping whitespace to O(1) per call. The rest of the parser remains unchanged, maintaining correctness while speeding up the initial parsing phase.
