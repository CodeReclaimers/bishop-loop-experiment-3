CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` is fundamentally flawed because `find()` returns the index of the *first* occurrence of any character in the whitespace set, not the index of the first non-whitespace character. This would incorrectly advance the position past the first whitespace character rather than skipping all leading whitespace. Additionally, `find()` is not O(1) and the repeated string concatenation of `_WS_CHARS` is inefficient.
