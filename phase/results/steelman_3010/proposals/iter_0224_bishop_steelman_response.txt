CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while-loop with `re.search` is flawed because `re.search` finds the *first* match, not the longest contiguous sequence of whitespace starting at the given position. This would incorrectly advance the position past only the first whitespace character or a partial sequence, breaking the whitespace-skipping logic. Additionally, it doesn't account for the fact that `re.search` is not necessarily faster than a simple loop for small inputs and could introduce overhead.
