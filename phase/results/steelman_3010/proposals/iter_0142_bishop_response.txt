Replace the call to `_skip_ws` in `parse` and `_parse_value` with `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` instead of invoking `_skip_ws`, and remove the compiled `_WS_PATTERN` altogether. This lets Python’s built‑in `str.find` skip all whitespace characters in a single operation, avoiding the overhead of the regex engine and the per‑call iteration in the current implementation.
