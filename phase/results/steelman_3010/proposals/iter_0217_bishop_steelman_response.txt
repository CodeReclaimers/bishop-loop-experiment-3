This would cause incorrect behavior when there's no whitespace, or when the regex engine matches a zero-length match and advances the position incorrectly. Additionally, it would not preserve the existing behavior of skipping exactly the whitespace characters, as the regex would not be anchored to skip only the leading whitespace.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use `re.match` or `re.search` with a pattern that finds the *first* non-whitespace character starting from `pos`, and then compute the index of that character.
