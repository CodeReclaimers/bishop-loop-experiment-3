In `json_parser.py` remove the unused `_WS_PATTERN` constant and its reference from `_skip_ws`, allowing the whitespace‑skipping routine to rely only on the precomputed `_WS_CHARS` set, which performs O(1) membership checks without the overhead of a compiled regex.
