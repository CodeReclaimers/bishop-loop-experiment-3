CRITIQUE: The suggestion to move translation-table construction to module level and reuse it in `_skip_ws` is unworkable because `str.translate()` with a translation table cannot be used to skip whitespace characters efficiently in this context. The approach would require constructing a translation table that maps whitespace to `None` or some sentinel, but then finding the next non-whitespace character would still require a linear scan or additional logic. Furthermore, the use of `find` after translation doesn't directly solve the problem of skipping a variable-length sequence of whitespace.
STEELMAN: A better approach is to precompute a translation table at module level that maps all whitespace characters to a single sentinel character (like `'\0'`), then use `str.translate()` to replace all whitespace with that sentinel, followed by `str.find()` to locate the next non-sentinel character. This avoids repeated construction of translation tables and allows fast skipping of sequences of whitespace.
