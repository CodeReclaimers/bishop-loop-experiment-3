CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the manual whitespace loop with a regex match would break correctness because the regex engine's `match` method only finds a match at the beginning of the string, not a contiguous sequence of whitespace characters starting from the given position. Additionally, it would not properly advance `pos` to the correct next non-whitespace character, as the regex match result doesn't directly tell you how many characters were consumed. This could lead to incorrect parsing or infinite loops.
STEELMAN: The steelman version would use `re.match` to find the longest contiguous sequence of whitespace starting at `pos`, then advance `pos` by the length of that match. This preserves the intent of leveraging regex for bulk skipping while maintaining correctness by properly updating the position.
