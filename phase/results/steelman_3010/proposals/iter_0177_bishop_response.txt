Use the precompiled regular expression _WS_PATTERN to locate and jump past all whitespace in one call instead of looping through each character. Modify _skip_ws so it runs pos = _WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos).end() when whitespace exists, otherwise leaves pos unchanged, which reduces the cost of whitespace skipping to a single O(1) regex match. This keeps correctness intact while dramatically speeding up the initial parse step.
