CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `re.search()` with a precompiled regex for whitespace skipping is flawed because `re.search()` scans forward until it finds the *first* non-matching character, which means it will not efficiently skip over long runs of whitespace. Additionally, it doesn't handle the case where the input is exhausted properly, and the regex engine may not be faster than a simple loop for short whitespace runs. The approach also introduces a performance regression in cases where there is little or no whitespace.
