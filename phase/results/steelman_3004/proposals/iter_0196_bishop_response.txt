Replace the per‑character `_skip_ws` loop that checks each character against `_WS_CHARS` with a binary search on a pre‑computed list of non‑whitespace indices; after building this list once, `_skip_ws` returns the next non‑whitespace position instantly with `bisect_right`, eliminating the O(k) scan per whitespace run.
