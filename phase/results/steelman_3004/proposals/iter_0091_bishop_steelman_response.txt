This would lead to incorrect parsing or infinite loops. Additionally, the regex approach is likely slower for small amounts of whitespace due to regex overhead.
STEELMAN: The steelman version would use `_WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos)` to find the next non-whitespace character, then advance the position to the match's start, effectively skipping all whitespace in one go.
