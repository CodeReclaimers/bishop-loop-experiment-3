Replace the character‑by‑character whitespace loop in `_skip_ws` with a direct index jump using a pre‑computed `next_non_ws` table; this turns each skip operation from O(k) where k is the number of consecutive whitespace characters into O(1) by returning the stored position of the next non‑whitespace character. Since the table is built once per parse, the overall parsing speed improves dramatically without altering the input‑output behavior.
