Additionally, regex matching is generally slower than a simple loop for small amounts of whitespace, and the overhead of regex compilation and state management may outweigh any gains in code simplicity.

This preserves the intent of using regex for efficient whitespace skipping while ensuring correctness by relying on the regex engine's ability to match maximal munch of the defined whitespace set.
