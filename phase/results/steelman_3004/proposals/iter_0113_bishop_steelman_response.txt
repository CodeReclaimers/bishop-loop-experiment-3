CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace `_skip_ws` with a regex-based approach would be unworkable because the regex `_WS_PATTERN` is compiled to match zero or more whitespace characters, not to jump to the next non-whitespace character. Using it in a loop would still require scanning through the input, negating the intended performance benefit. Additionally, the regex approach would likely introduce overhead from regex compilation and matching that outweighs any gains from avoiding the while-loop.

STEELMAN: The steelman approach would be to use the precomputed `_WS_PATTERN` to skip whitespace by matching from the current position and advancing the index by the match length, effectively skipping over all whitespace in one operation rather than checking each character individually.
