CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `pattern.search(text, pos).end()` directly is flawed because it doesn't handle the case where the regex doesn't match (i.e., no whitespace at all) and would require additional logic to detect that. Also, `search()` finds the first match, not necessarily the longest sequence of whitespace starting at `pos`, which could lead to incorrect parsing. It also doesn't account for the fact that the regex engine may not be faster than a simple loop for small amounts of whitespace.

STEELMAN: A better approach would be to use `pattern.match(text, pos)` to find a contiguous sequence of whitespace starting exactly at `pos`, and if it matches, advance the position by the matched length; otherwise, return `pos` unchanged. This preserves the intent of leveraging regex for efficiency while maintaining correctness and avoiding Python-level loops.
