Replace the manual whitespace‑skipping loop in `_skip_ws` with a lookup into the pre‑computed `skip_table`, so `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_array`, and `_parse_string` call `pos = skip_table[pos]` instead of iterating through characters.
