Replace the compiled `_WS_PATTERN` regex with the simple `_WS_CHARS` set lookup used in `_skip_ws`, eliminating the regex match overhead entirely. This means removing `re.compile(r'[ \t\n\r]*)` and the `WS_PATTERN` variable, so `_skip_ws` continues to advance `pos` by checking `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS` directly.
