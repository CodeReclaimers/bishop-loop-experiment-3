CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the manual loop with `regex.search()` is flawed because it doesn't account for the fact that `regex.search()` finds the *first* match, not all consecutive whitespace characters starting at `pos`. Additionally, it ignores that the current implementation advances `pos` character-by-character and expects to return the position *after* the whitespace, which `search()` alone cannot directly provide without additional logic.
