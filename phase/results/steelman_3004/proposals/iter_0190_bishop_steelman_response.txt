In practice, building the list takes O(n) time and the overhead of binary search (cache misses, branching) likely outweighs the gains unless whitespace runs are extremely long. Additionally, the approach doesn't account for the fact that `_skip_ws` is called frequently and may only skip a few characters at a time, making the binary search inefficient.
