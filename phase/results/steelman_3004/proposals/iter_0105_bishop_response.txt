Replace the character‑by‑character loop in `_skip_ws` with a direct lookup into the pre‑computed `ws_table`, so each call jumps to the next non‑whitespace position in O(1) time instead of O(k) where k is the length of the whitespace run. Keep the `ws_table` built once at the start of `parse` and remove `_skip_ws` entirely to avoid re‑scanning characters repeatedly. This eliminates repeated traversal of whitespace, which is the dominant cost in the parser.
