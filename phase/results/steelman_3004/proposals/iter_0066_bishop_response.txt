Replace the per‑character whitespace skipping in `parse` and `_parse_value` with a single call to the pre‑compiled `_WS_PATTERN` using `re.search(...).start()` to jump past all whitespace in one O(1) operation instead of looping through each character. This eliminates the overhead of the while‑loop in `_skip_ws` and the repeated membership checks, while still maintaining the same logical result.
