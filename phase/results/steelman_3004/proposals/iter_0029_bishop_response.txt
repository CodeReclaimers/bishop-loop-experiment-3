Replace the manual character‑by‑character whitespace check in the `while` loop of `_skip_ws` with a direct call to the compiled regex `_WS_PATTERN`, which can skip the entire run of whitespace in a single operation and return the next non‑whitespace index. This avoids the repeated `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS` loop, reducing the cost of whitespace skipping from O(k) per call to O(1) per call, while keeping the same end‑point detection logic.
