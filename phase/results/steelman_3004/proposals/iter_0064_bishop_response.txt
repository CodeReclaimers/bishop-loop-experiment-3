Replace the manual whitespace‑skipping loop in `_skip_ws` with a call to the pre‑compiled regex `_WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos, stop=len(text))` and return its end position, so whitespace is skipped in a single regex operation instead of iterating character‑by‑character.
