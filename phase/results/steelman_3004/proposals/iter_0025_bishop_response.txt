Replace the iterative whitespace‑skipping loop in `_skip_ws` with a direct call to the compiled `_WS_PATTERN` using `pattern.start(pos)` and `pattern.end()` (or using `pattern.span(pos).end()`), which will jump past all whitespace in one regex evaluation instead of character‑by‑character increments.
