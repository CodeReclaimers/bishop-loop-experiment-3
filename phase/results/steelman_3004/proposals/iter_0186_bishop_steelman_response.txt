CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use binary search on a pre-computed list of non-whitespace positions is fundamentally flawed because it requires building and maintaining an expensive auxiliary data structure that doesn't scale well with input size. It also ignores the fact that in typical JSON parsing, whitespace is often minimal or localized, making the overhead of binary search worse than linear scanning. Additionally, the approach doesn't account for the fact that whitespace skipping is usually a small fraction of total parsing time, so the optimization may not yield meaningful performance gains.
