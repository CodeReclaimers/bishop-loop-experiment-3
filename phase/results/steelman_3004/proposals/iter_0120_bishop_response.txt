Replace the manual character‑by‑character whitespace skips in _skip_ws and the digit‑loop inside _parse_number with direct use of the compiled _WS_PATTERN for whitespace and _NUMBER_PATTERN for numbers, so the parser can jump past all whitespace or the full numeric token in one step instead of iterating over each character. This eliminates repeated loops and reduces memory overhead from maintaining a full whitespace table, giving a measurable speed gain while keeping correctness.
