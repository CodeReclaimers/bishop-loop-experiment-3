Replace the current `_skip_ws` implementation that builds and searches a list of whitespace indices with a simple loop that advances `pos` while the character at `pos` is in `_WS_CHARS`. This removes the overhead of maintaining a separate whitespace‑index list and the binary‑search logic, so the function runs in O(k) time where k is the number of consecutive whitespace characters, which is the natural cost and is faster than the added complexity.
