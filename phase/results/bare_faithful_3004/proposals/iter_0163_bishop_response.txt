Replace the repeated calls to _WS_PATTERN.match inside _parse_value, _parse_object, _parse_array, _parse_string and _parse_bool with a simple while‑loop that increments pos while text[pos] is in _WS_CHARS, removing the overhead of building and matching the compiled regex each time.
