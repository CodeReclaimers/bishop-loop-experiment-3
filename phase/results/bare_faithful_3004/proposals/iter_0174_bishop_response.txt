Replace the regex‑based `_parse_number` call in `_parse_value` with a hand‑rolled loop that consumes digits (and optional sign, decimal point and exponent) using the pre‑computed `_DIGIT_CHARS`, `_HEX_CHARS` and `_ESCAPE_CHARS` sets, directly advancing the index instead of invoking `re.match`. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and matching a regular expression for every number and reduces memory allocation. By processing the substring manually, the parser stays correct while gaining a constant‑time scan that is generally faster than regex matching.
