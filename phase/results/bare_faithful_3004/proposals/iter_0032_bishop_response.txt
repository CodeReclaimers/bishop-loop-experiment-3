Replace every occurrence of the regex call “_WS_PATTERN.match(text, pos).end()” with the inline whitespace‑skipping routine “pos = _skip_ws(text, pos)” inside the *parse* function and its helper _parse_object; this removes the overhead of recompiling and matching a pattern for each character and instead uses the O(1) set‑based check that _skip_ws already performs.
