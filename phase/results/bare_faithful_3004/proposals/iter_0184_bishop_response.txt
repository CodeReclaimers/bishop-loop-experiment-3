Replace the `_skip_ws` function’s use of the compiled regular‑expression `_WS_PATTERN` with a plain character‑by‑character loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos]` is in the `_WS_CHARS` set. This removes the regex matching overhead in the tight inner loop, making whitespace skipping O(1) per character rather than O(k) per match. The rest of the parser already uses the `_WS_CHARS` set for direct membership checks, so the change keeps correctness intact while speeding up parsing.
