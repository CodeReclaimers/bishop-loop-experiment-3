Replace the current `_parse_number` implementation that relies on a compiled regex `_NUMBER_PATTERN` with a hand‑written loop that uses the pre‑computed `_DIGIT_CHARS` and `_HEX_CHARS` sets to scan forward until a non‑digit (or a non‑hex character) is found, then immediately construct the integer or float. This eliminates the regex match overhead and the string‑concatenation steps, providing O(1) lookups for early termination and avoiding the regex engine entirely.
