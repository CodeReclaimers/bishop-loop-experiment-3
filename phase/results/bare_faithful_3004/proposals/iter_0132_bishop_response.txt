Replace the whitespace‑skipping calls that rely on the regular‑expression `_skip_ws` (used in `_parse_object`, `_parse_value`, and the outer whitespace loop) with a direct byte‑wise loop `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1`. This eliminates the overhead of the regex match and its internal state‑machine, which is O(1) per character without the regex engine’s per‑call cost. The change preserves correctness because the same set `_WS_CHARS` is still used for the membership test.
