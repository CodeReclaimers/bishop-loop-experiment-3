Replace the repeated character‑by‑character loops that advance `pos` and check `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS` with a single call to `_WS_PATTERN.match(text, pos)` to obtain the new position directly; this eliminates the overhead of many Python iterations and still correctly skips all whitespace.
