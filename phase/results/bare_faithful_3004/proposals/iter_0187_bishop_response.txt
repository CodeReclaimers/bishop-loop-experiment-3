Replace the character‑by‑character whitespace‑skipping loops in _skip_ws, _parse_object, _parse_array, and _skip_ws‑based whitespace checks (e.g., the while loops around `pos += 1` that manually advance over `_WS_CHARS`) with calls to `_skip_ws`, which consumes whitespace in a single compiled‑regex operation instead of thousands of Python‑level increments.
