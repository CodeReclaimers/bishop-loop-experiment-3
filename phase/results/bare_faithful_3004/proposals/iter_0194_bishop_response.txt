Replace the `_skip_ws` helper that calls the compiled regex each time with a simple loop that increments `pos` while `text[pos]` is in the `_WS_CHARS` frozenset. This eliminates the regex overhead per whitespace skip while keeping the same O‑length behavior. The change applies only to the `_skip_ws` call inside `_parse_value` and `_parse_object`, making the parser faster without altering correctness.
