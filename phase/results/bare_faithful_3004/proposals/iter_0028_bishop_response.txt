Replace the calls to _WS_PATTERN.span in parse and _parse_value with a direct call to _skip_ws (pos) that iterates over _WS_CHARS instead of invoking a compiled regex, eliminating the O(log n) regex overhead each time whitespace is skipped. This change keeps the same O(1) character‑by‑character check but removes the regex compilation cost, yielding a measurable speedup.
