Replace every call to `_skip_ws` that uses the compiled `_WS_PATTERN` with a simple inline `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loop. This eliminates the overhead of matching and moving the regex engine’s state each time, turning the O(k) regex call into O(k) direct character checks which is faster. The logic remains identical, so correctness is preserved.
