Replace the whitespace‑skipping logic in `_parse_object` that currently uses `_WS_PATTERN.match` to advance the position with a direct loop that checks `_WS_CHARS`, eliminating the regex match call. This removes the overhead of compiling and matching the regex on every key‑delimiter encounter while preserving the same O(1) character‑set lookup for whitespace. The rest of the parser can keep the pre‑computed `_WS_CHARS` set for fast membership tests.
