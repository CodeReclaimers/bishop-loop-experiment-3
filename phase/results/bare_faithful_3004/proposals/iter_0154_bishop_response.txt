Replace the character‑by‑character whitespace skipping in `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_array`, and related helper calls with a single call to the pre‑compiled `_WS_PATTERN` regex, setting `pos = m.end()` instead of looping over each whitespace character. This eliminates the Python loop overhead and the repeated `in _WS_CHARS` checks, letting the compiled regex move the cursor in O(1) time per segment. The resulting parser still skips whitespace correctly and maintains the same correctness guarantees.
