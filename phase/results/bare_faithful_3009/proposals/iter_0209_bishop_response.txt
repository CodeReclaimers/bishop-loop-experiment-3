Replace the regex‑based whitespace skips in `parse`, `_parse_object`, and `_parse_array` with calls to the already‑defined `_skip_ws` function, which iterates directly over characters instead of invoking a compiled regex each time. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching for every whitespace segment and lets the parser advance the index in a single linear‑time pass. The change keeps correctness because `_skip_ws` already handles any number of leading whitespace characters without side effects.
