Replace the `_skip_ws` function, which currently uses a compiled regex to advance the position, with a simple loop that checks each character against the pre‑computed `_WS_CHARS` set, advancing `pos` one step at a time. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching while keeping the O(1) membership test and preserving correctness. The change is limited to the whitespace‑skipping logic and does not touch any other parsing code.
