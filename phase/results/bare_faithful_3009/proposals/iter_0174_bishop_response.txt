Replace the calls to the regular‑expression `_WS_PATTERN` in `_skip_ws` and the early whitespace‑skip in `parse` with a simple loop that checks `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS` and increments `pos`. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and matching the regex for every whitespace segment, using only O(1) set membership tests. The rest of the parser stays unchanged, preserving correctness while gaining speed.
