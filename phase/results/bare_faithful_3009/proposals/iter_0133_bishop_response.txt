Replace the regex‑based `_skip_ws` and whitespace‑skipping loops in `parse` and `_parse_value` with a simple while‑loop that checks `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS`, which uses the O(1) membership test of the frozenset `_WS_CHARS` instead of calling the compiled pattern each time. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching and directly advances the pointer, making the parser faster while preserving correctness.
