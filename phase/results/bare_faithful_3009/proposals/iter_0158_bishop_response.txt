Replace the regex‑based whitespace skipping in both `parse` and `_skip_ws` with a simple numeric loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos] in _WS_CHARS`, avoiding the cost of matching and capturing groups. This removes the `re` module and its internal state handling, letting Python’s fast C‑level loop run directly over the string characters. The resulting pointer updates are O(1) per character, matching the O(1) membership test of `_WS_CHARS` without regex overhead.
