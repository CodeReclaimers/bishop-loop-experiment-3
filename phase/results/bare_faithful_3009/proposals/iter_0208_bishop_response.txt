Replace the call to `_WS_PATTERN.match` inside `_parse_object` and `_parse_value` with a direct call to `_skip_ws`, which simply advances the position by looping over whitespace characters instead of invoking a compiled regular expression. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching and reduces memory allocation for each whitespace skip. The function `_skip_ws` already performs the same operation efficiently, so this change keeps correctness intact while improving speed.
