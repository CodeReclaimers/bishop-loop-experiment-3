Replace every call to the compiled regex _WS_PATTERN.match in the parse‑level whitespace skipping and in _parse_object’s whitespace handling with the inlined _skip_ws function, which simply iterates and tests _WS_CHARS in a tight loop without the overhead of regular‑expression compilation. This eliminates the per‑iteration regex engine work and reduces the per‑character cost from O(log m) to O(1) per character, while keeping the same O(1) character‑set lookup.
