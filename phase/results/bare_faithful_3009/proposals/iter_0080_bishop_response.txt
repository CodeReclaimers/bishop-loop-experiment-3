Replace the character‑by‑character loop in `_skip_ws` that increments `pos` one whitespace character at a time with a single call to the pre‑compiled `_WS_PATTERN` (e.g., using `match` or `search`) that consumes the entire run of whitespace in one go, which eliminates the O(n) per‑character checks and makes whitespace skipping much faster. This change keeps correctness intact because the same whitespace is skipped, just in a single step.
