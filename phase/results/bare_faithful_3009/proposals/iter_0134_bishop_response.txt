Replace the regex‑based whitespace skipping in `_skip_ws` and in `parse` with a direct loop that checks `pos` against the precomputed frozenset `_WS_CHARS`, eliminating the overhead of `re.match`. The loop will advance `pos` while `pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS`, which is O(1) per character and avoids recompiling or searching the pattern each time. This targeted change speeds up whitespace skipping without affecting correctness.
