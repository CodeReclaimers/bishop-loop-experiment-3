Replace the `_skip_ws` function’s current use of the compiled regex `_WS_PATTERN` with a simple `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loop, since `_WS_CHARS` is a frozenset and membership testing is O(1).
