Replace the use of the compiled regular‑expression _WS_PATTERN throughout the parser with direct iteration over the pre‑computed frozenset _WS_CHARS, so whitespace‑skipping is done with the fast set‑membership check instead of regex matching. This eliminates the overhead of compiling and executing the regex in tight loops while keeping the same O(1) character test. The result is a faster, equally correct whitespace‑skipping mechanism.
