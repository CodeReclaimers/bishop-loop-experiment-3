Replace the current `_skip_ws` implementation that uses the compiled regex `_WS_PATTERN.match` with a straightforward while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is in the pre‑computed `_WS_CHARS` set. This eliminates the overhead of regex matching and makes whitespace skipping O(1) per character. Since the loop already exits at the first non‑whitespace character, the behavior and correctness remain unchanged.
