Replace the whitespace‑skipping blocks that use `_WS_PATTERN.match` in `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_array` and `_parse_string` with a direct `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1` loop. This eliminates the regex engine call per skip, giving O(1) character checks instead of O(k) regex work, while the same logic advances `pos` correctly.
