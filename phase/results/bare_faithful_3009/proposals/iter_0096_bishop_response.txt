Replace the _skip_ws function’s use of the _WS_PATTERN regex with a direct loop that advances pos while text[pos] is in _WS_CHARS, and remove the compiled _WS_PATTERN entirely, so whitespace skipping is done with the O(1) set‑membership test instead of regex matching. This eliminates the regex compilation cost and the overhead of matching each call, speeding up repeated skips without altering the parsing logic.
