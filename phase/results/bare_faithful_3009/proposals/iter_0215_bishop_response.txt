Replace all calls to `_WS_PATTERN.match` inside the parser with the dedicated `_skip_ws` function, which advances the position pointer by iterating through the whitespace characters directly. This eliminates the overhead of recompiling the regex on each call and reduces the per‑character cost to a simple loop, yielding faster whitespace skipping. The correctness is unchanged because `_skip_ws` correctly consumes any sequence of whitespace characters up to the next non‑whitespace character.
