To improve performance, we can use a more efficient way to skip whitespace by utilizing a generator expression instead of the `re` module's `match` function in `_skip_ws`. This reduces the overhead of regex matching and improves the speed of the parser. We also avoid using string concatenation directly in the `_parse_object` function, which is slower than appending to a list.
