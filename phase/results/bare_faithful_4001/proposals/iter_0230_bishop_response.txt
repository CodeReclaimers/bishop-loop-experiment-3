To optimize the `json_parser.py` for speed, we can consider using an iterative approach instead of recursion. This change will allow us to parse JSON more efficiently by processing characters one by one without the overhead of recursive function calls.

Here's why this change is beneficial:

1. **Reduced Recursion Depth**: Iterative parsing avoids deep recursion stacks, which can lead to stack overflow for large JSON objects.
2. **Improved Performance**: By processing each character sequentially, we avoid the overhead of setting up and managing a call stack, resulting in faster execution times.

The specific change involves replacing recursive calls with iterative loops throughout the codebase. This approach ensures that the parser maintains its correctness while improving its performance.
