Replace the per‑iteration dict rebuild in `_parse_object` (the line `out = dict(out); out[k] = v`) with a plain dictionary that is created once and populated with `out[key] = value`, eliminating the costly `dict(out)` call. Likewise, in `_parse_string` replace the repeated `out = out + [c]` with an `out.append(c)` so the characters are stored in a list and joined only once at the end of parsing. This avoids repeated string concatenations and dict construction, which are the main source of the slowdown.
