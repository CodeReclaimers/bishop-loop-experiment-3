Replace the per‑call character‑by‑character whitespace skip (`_skip_ws`) with a binary‑search lookup that jumps directly to the next non‑whitespace position using a pre‑computed list of indices of non‑whitespace characters. This eliminates the linear scan of the input string at every step, turning whitespace skipping from O(n) to O(log m) where m is the number of non‑whitespace tokens.
