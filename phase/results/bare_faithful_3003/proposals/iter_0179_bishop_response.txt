Replace the per‑call `_skip_ws` loop that scans whitespace characters with a single binary‑search lookup on a pre‑computed list of non‑whitespace character indices built once at the start of `parse`; then use that list to jump directly to the next non‑whitespace position. This removes the O(n) per‑call whitespace scanning and, because the list is built once, the parsing remains correct while the time per parse drops.
