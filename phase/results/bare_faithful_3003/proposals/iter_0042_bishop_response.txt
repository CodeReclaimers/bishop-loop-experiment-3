Replace the character‑by‑character loop inside _skip_ws that advances pos while text[pos] is in _WS_CHARS with a single call to str.find or find_first_of to locate the next whitespace character, moving the index directly to the following non‑whitespace position. This eliminates the O(k) per‑call cost of scanning each whitespace character and instead performs a constant‑time lookup, making the parser much faster while preserving correctness.
