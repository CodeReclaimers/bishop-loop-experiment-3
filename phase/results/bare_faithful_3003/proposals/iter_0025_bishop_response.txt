Replace the repeated string and dict concatenation in `_parse_object` and `_parse_array` with in‑place updates: use `out.append(v)` and `out[k] = v` instead of recreating `out = out + [v]` or `out = dict(out); out[k] = v` at each step, which eliminates the O(n²) cost of building new lists and dictionaries on every iteration.
