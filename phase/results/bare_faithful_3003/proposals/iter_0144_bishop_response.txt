In `_parse_object` replace the line that rebuilds the dictionary with a direct assignment `out[k] = v` instead of `out = dict(out); out[k] = v`, and in `_parse_array` replace `out = out + [v]` with `out.append(v)` so the parser no longer creates new dict objects or temporary lists at each iteration. This eliminates the repeated object construction and string concatenation that dominate the runtime.
