Replace the per‑call whitespace‑skipping loop that walks the string character‑by‑character with a binary‑search lookup based on a pre‑computed list of non‑whitespace character indices. This eliminates the O(n) scan in `_skip_ws` and reduces overhead to O(log m) where m is the number of non‑whitespace characters. The change should be applied only to the top‑level `parse` function and its calls to `_skip_ws`, leaving the public entry point unchanged.
