Replace the per‑item string concatenation and repeated rebuilding in `_parse_object` (and similarly `_parse_array`) by first collecting the key‑value pairs in a list or a list of `(k, v)` tuples and then constructing the final `dict` or list with a single comprehension or `dict.fromkeys`, which eliminates the O(n²) cost of repeated `out = out + …` operations while preserving correctness. This reduces the number of intermediate string creations and keeps the public `parse` entry point unchanged.
