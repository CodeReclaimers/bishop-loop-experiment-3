Replace the string‑concatenation‑based rebuilding of arrays and dictionaries in _parse_array and _parse_object with in‑place appends and direct dict assignment (e.g., out.append(v) and out[k]=v instead of out=out+[v] or out=dict(out); out[k]=v). This removes the O(n²) cost of repeatedly creating new lists and dicts, while the same logical flow remains unchanged, so the parser stays correct.
