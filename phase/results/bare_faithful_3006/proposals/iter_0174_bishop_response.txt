Replace all character‑by‑character whitespace checks (e.g., `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS:`) with a call to `_skip_ws(text, pos)`, which uses `text.find` to jump directly to the next non‑whitespace character. This single call skips an entire block of whitespace in O(1) time instead of O(k) where k is the length of the whitespace run, making the parser faster while still producing the same result.
