Replace the repetitive while loops that advance pos by one character at a time with a single call to _skip_ws (pos) to jump directly to the next non‑whitespace character, thereby eliminating the overhead of each individual iteration. This change applies to all whitespace‑skipping sections in _parse_value, _parse_object, _parse_array, _parse_string, _parse_bool, _parse_null, and _parse_number, using _skip_ws instead of the while loop.
