Replace the per‑character whitespace‑skipping loop in `_skip_ws` and the repeated manual checks throughout the parser with a single call to `_WS_RE.sub('', text)` at the start of `parse`, which collapses all whitespace in one regex pass. This removes the need for the `skip_table` and O(1) lookups, letting the parser work on a compact string and reducing the number of string operations and condition checks.
