Replace the while‑loop in _skip_ws that increments pos one character at a time with a call to the pre‑compiled _WS_RE matching the whitespace run, which returns the index of the first non‑whitespace character in O(1) time. This eliminates the O(k) cost of the loop where k is the number of whitespace characters skipped. The rest of the parser can keep using the same pos returned by the regex match.
