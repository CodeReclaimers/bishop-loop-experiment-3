Replace the current character‑by‑character whitespace loop in `_skip_ws` with a single call to the compiled regex `_WS_RE.search(text, pos).start()` to jump to the next non‑whitespace position, which eliminates the O(k) scan and replaces it with an O(1) regex operation.
