Replace the per‑character whitespace checks inside `_skip_ws` with a pre‑computed skip table that directly yields the next non‑whitespace index, eliminating the loop and its repeated set‑membership lookups. Since the table stores the next position for each index in O(1) time, the overall parser avoids the overhead of scanning each whitespace character individually. This change keeps correctness because the same logical outcome (skipping whitespace) is achieved, just faster.
