The regex approach also introduces overhead from regex compilation and matching that may not be beneficial in practice.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use `re.match()` or `re.search()` with the precompiled whitespace regex to find the end of the whitespace sequence in one operation, then advance the position by that amount.
