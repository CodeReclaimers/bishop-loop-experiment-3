It also ignores that many escapes are single characters (like `\n`, `\t`) and would require a mixed lookup strategy. Additionally, it doesn't address the performance benefit of avoiding repeated string slicing and list operations in the current implementation.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to pre-compute a dictionary mapping common single-character escapes (like `\n`, `\t`) to their Unicode values, and handle multi-character escapes like `\u1234` separately using a dedicated function that parses the hex digits.
