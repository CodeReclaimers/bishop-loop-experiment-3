CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `_WS_RE.span('')[1]` directly is flawed because `re.match` or `re.search` with `span()` doesn't advance the position correctly in the context of a streaming parser. It would require re-parsing the same text from the start, negating performance gains. Also, `span('')` is invalid — `span()` takes a group name or number, and group 0 is the entire match, not a specific capture group.

STEELMAN: The steelman is to use `re.match(_WS_RE, text, pos)` to find the next non-whitespace character, then advance the position to the end of the match. This preserves the intent of skipping whitespace efficiently in one operation while correctly advancing the parser's position.
