CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the manual whitespace loop with a regex call is problematic because `re.search()` starts matching from the given position but doesn't guarantee it will match from that exact point; it may match at a later position. Additionally, `re.search()` is not optimized for skipping whitespace at a specific location — it's designed for pattern matching, not efficient forward skipping. This could introduce performance regressions or incorrect behavior when whitespace is not at the start of the input.

STEELMAN: A better approach would be to use `re.match()` or `re.fullmatch()` with a regex that matches zero or more whitespace characters starting from the given position, and then advance the position by the matched length.
