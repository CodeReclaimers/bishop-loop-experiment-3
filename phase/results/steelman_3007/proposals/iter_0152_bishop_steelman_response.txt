CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the character-by-character loop with a single regex match would not work correctly because `re.match()` only matches at the beginning of the string, not from a given position. Additionally, it would not properly handle the case where the regex engine might not advance the position correctly in all scenarios, potentially leading to infinite loops or incorrect parsing. The regex approach also doesn't take advantage of the fact that whitespace characters are known and simple, making a direct loop more efficient and predictable.
