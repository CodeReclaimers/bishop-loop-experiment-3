It also fails to account for the fact that whitespace is not uniformly distributed and must be skipped at specific points to maintain correct parsing state. Additionally, this approach would not handle escaped strings or nested structures properly.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use the regex to pre-skip leading whitespace once at the start of parsing, but still perform localized whitespace skipping at critical junctures like after commas, colons, and braces/brackets to maintain proper parsing boundaries.
