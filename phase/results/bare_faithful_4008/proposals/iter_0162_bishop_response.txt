An optimization idea is to use an explicit stack for tracking the opening braces and closing braces, which can help in reducing the need for multiple recursive calls and improve performance. This change involves using a `stack` list to keep track of the positions of `{` and `}` characters. When encountering a `{`, it pushes its position onto the stack; when encountering a `}`, it pops the last position from the stack, indicating that the corresponding opening brace has been matched. This approach avoids recursive calls and reduces the overhead associated with function calls.
