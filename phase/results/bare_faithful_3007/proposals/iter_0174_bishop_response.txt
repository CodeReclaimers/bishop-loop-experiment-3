Replace the current `_skip_ws` function’s while‑loop that scans whitespace one character at a time with a direct lookup into the pre‑computed `_build_next_non_ws_table`, which already stores, for each position, the index of the next non‑whitespace character. By removing the loop and using `pos = _next_non_ws_table[pos]` (or the equivalent) you eliminate O(k) work per whitespace span and achieve O(1) per call, while the table is built once at parse time. This change speeds up every whitespace‑skipping operation in the parser without altering the logic or correctness.
