Replace the extensive conditional loops in `_parse_number` with a single regex match that consumes the whole numeric literal at once, advancing `pos` to the end of the match and returning the parsed value directly. This removes the repeated checks for digits, the decimal point, and the exponent, cutting thousands of string‑index operations per number. The regex can be compiled once (e.g., `_NUM_RE = re.compile(r'[+-]?(\d+\.?\d*|\.\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\d+)?')`) and used to parse both ints and floats in one call.
