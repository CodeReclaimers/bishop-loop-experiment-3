Replace the incremental `pos` increments in `_parse_number` with a single `str.find` that locates the last allowed character by matching the digit, optional fraction, and optional exponent pattern against `_DIGIT_CHARS` and `_HEX_CHARS`. This eliminates the repeated `while` loops that advance `pos` one character at a time, instead jumping directly to the end of the number, cutting loop overhead and improving speed.
