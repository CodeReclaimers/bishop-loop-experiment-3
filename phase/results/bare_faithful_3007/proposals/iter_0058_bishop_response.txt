Replace the per‑character digit checks in `_parse_number` with a single call to the pre‑computed regular expression `_NUM_RE` to locate the end of the number, advancing `pos` directly instead of stepping through each character. This eliminates the loop over every digit, reducing work from O(k) to O(1) for the integer part, while the regex still validates the same numeric format for correctness. The rest of the parser remains unchanged, so no new bugs are introduced.
