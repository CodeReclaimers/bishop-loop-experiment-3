Replace the series of `if‑elif` checks in `_parse_string` that handle escaped characters and Unicode escapes with a single dictionary lookup that maps each possible escape sequence to its corresponding value, so the parser can resolve escapes in constant‑time rather than through a chain of conditional branches. This eliminates the branching overhead and reduces the number of string‑index operations while preserving the exact same output. The change is isolated to the string‑parsing loop and does not affect any other part of the parser.
