In `_skip_ws`, replace the per‑character loop that checks each character against `_WS_CHARS` with a series of `find` calls that jump directly to the next occurrence of each whitespace character, using the pre‑computed sets to compute offsets and skip over blocks of whitespace in one step. This eliminates the repeated character‑by‑character checks and reduces the number of iterations, especially on large inputs, while still achieving O(1) membership look‑ups.
