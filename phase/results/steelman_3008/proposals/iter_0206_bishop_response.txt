The `_skip_ws` function iterates character‑by‑character over the whitespace set, incurring O(k) cost per call where *k* is the length of the whitespace block. Replacing that with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` returns the index of the first non‑whitespace character in O(1) (or linear‑time over the whole string), eliminating the inner loop and dramatically reducing overhead. This change speeds up whitespace skipping without altering correctness.
