CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while loop with `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` is flawed because `find()` only returns the position of the first occurrence of any whitespace character, not the end position of a contiguous whitespace block. This would still require a loop to advance past all whitespace, negating the benefit. Additionally, `find()` with a multi-character string is not O(1) and can be slower than the current set-based approach for small whitespace runs.
