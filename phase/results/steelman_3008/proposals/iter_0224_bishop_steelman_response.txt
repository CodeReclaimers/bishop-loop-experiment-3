CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.translate` on the entire input string would break the parser's ability to track accurate positions through the original text, since the translated string would have different lengths and character indices. It also fails to account for the fact that whitespace skipping is needed at multiple points in the parsing process, not just once at the start, and would require complex position mapping logic to maintain correctness. Additionally, the performance benefit of avoiding set membership checks is negligible compared to the overhead of translating the entire string and managing position tracking.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to precompute a translation table that maps whitespace characters to a single sentinel character (like `'\x00'`), then use `str.translate` once at the beginning to compact the input.
