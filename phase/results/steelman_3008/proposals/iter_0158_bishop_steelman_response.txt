CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.find` for digit sequences is flawed because `str.find` searches for the first occurrence of a substring, not the end of a contiguous sequence. It would not correctly identify where a digit sequence ends, especially when mixed with decimal points or exponents. Additionally, it fails to handle negative numbers or complex number formats properly, since `str.find` doesn't account for the structure of valid number syntax.

STEELMAN: A better approach would be to use `str.index` or a regex-based method to find the first non-digit character after the initial digit sequence, allowing us to efficiently skip over the entire number without a per-character loop. This preserves the performance goal while maintaining correctness for all valid number formats including negatives, decimals, and exponents.
