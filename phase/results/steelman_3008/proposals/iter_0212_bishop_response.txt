Replace the iterative while‑loop in _skip_ws with a single call to text.find(_WS_STR, pos), which can jump to the next non‑whitespace character in one operation instead of stepping through each character; this eliminates the per‑character loop and reduces overhead while preserving the same whitespace‑skip semantics. The change is limited to _skip_ws, and because find returns the exact index of the first non‑whitespace character, correctness is unchanged.
