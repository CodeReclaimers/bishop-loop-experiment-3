Replace the repeated string‑concatenation and dictionary recreation in `_parse_object` with a single list of `(key, value)` tuples that is built during parsing, then construct the final dictionary with `dict(zip(keys, values))`; this avoids the costly `out = out + [v]` and `out[key] = v` operations and eliminates the constant‑time per‑item dictionary reallocation. This single transformation reduces the per‑item build cost from O(1) string concatenation to O(1) list append, which is asymptotically faster and keeps the parser correct.
