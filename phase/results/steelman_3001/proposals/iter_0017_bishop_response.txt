Replace the repeated string concatenation in the parser (e.g., `out = out + c` inside `_parse_string` and `_parse_object`) with a list‑based accumulation that is joined at the end, such as `out.append(c)` followed by `return ''.join(out)` or `return dict(out)` after the loop. This eliminates the O(n²) cost of building strings with `+` and reduces the per‑character overhead to O(1). The change keeps correctness because the same characters are emitted in order and the final join produces the exact string.
