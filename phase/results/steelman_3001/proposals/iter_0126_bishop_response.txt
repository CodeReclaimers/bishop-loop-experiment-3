Replace the character‑by‑character whitespace skipping loop in `_skip_ws` with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` to locate the next non‑whitespace character, then advance `pos` to that index; this reduces the O(k) loop to an O(1) operation per skip. The change keeps the same semantics because `find` returns the index of the first non‑whitespace character, matching the original loop’s behavior.
