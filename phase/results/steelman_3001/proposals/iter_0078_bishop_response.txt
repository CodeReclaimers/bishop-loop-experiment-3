Replace the character‑by‑character loop in `_parse_number` with a single call to `text.find` that locates the end of the numeric token by scanning forward until a character outside `_DIGIT_CHARS` is found, then slice the substring and advance `pos` once instead of iterating each digit individually. This reduces the per‑number work from O(k) iterations to a single O(k) find operation, which is asymptotically faster for long numbers.
