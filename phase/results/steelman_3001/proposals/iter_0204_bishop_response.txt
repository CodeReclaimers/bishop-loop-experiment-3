Replace the character‑by‑character loop in `_skip_ws` with a single call to `str.find` that uses a generator expression to locate the next non‑whitespace character: `pos = text.find(next((c for c in text[pos:] if c not in _WS_CHARS), len(text)))` which skips all whitespace in one pass. This avoids the per‑iteration overhead of the `while` loop and still runs in O(k) where k is the number of skipped characters.
