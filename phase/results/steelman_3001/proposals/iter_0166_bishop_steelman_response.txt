CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace character-by-character digit scanning with `str.find` is unworkable because `str.find` cannot locate the end of a numeric token reliably—numbers can contain multiple components (integer, fractional, exponential) separated by '.', 'e', or 'E', and `str.find` would not account for these structured delimiters. Additionally, the approach fails to handle negative signs, decimal points, and scientific notation properly without complex additional logic, making it brittle and incorrect.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use `str.find` to locate the first non-digit character after the start of a number, but still process the number components (sign, decimal point, exponent) explicitly to ensure correctness, while avoiding the inner loop over digits. This maintains the performance benefit of reducing per-character checks while preserving correctness.
