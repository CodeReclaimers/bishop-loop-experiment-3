CRITIQUE: The suggestion to pre-scan and build token spans would break the recursive descent parsing structure, since the parser needs to advance through the input incrementally and handle whitespace at every grammar boundary. It would also require significant refactoring of the entire parsing logic to work with precomputed token positions instead of streaming parsing. Additionally, the approach doesn't consider that whitespace skipping is only needed at specific grammar points, not throughout the entire input.
