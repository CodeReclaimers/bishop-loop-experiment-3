This can be achieved by maintaining a character counter (`cpos`) that starts at `pos` and increments every time a non-whitespace character is found. By comparing `cpos` with the current position (`pos`), we can determine whether to skip whitespace or move ahead directly, which reduces the overhead of calling `isspace()`.
