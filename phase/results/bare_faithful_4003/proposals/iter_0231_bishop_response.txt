To improve performance in the JSON parser, one specific change that could be made is to use a stack-based approach for whitespace skipping instead of using a generator expression. This would allow the function to maintain a stack of positions where it has encountered spaces and resume parsing from those points when it encounters another space, reducing redundant work.
