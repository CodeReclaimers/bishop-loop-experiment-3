To speed up the JSON parser without compromising correctness, we can use a stack-based approach for whitespace handling instead of relying on regular expressions for skipping spaces. This will reduce the overhead of regex operations and improve performance significantly. The key is to maintain a stack of positions where whitespace was found during parsing, and when encountering a non-whitespace character, pop the last whitespace position from the stack until we reach the start of the next token (e.g., `{`, `[`, `"`, `t`, `f`, `n`, or number). This way, we can avoid unnecessary regex matching and improve the parser's efficiency.
