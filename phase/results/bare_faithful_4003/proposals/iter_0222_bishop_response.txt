To optimize the `json_parser.py` for performance, we can use a stack-based approach for whitespace skipping instead of a regular expression. This will reduce the overhead of compiling and running regular expressions, which can be time-consuming for large inputs. The approach works by iterating through the string and pushing positions to a stack whenever a whitespace character is found. When a non-whitespace character is encountered, the stack is popped to resume the parsing from the previous position. This allows us to efficiently skip over sequences of whitespace characters without performing regex checks at every step, resulting in faster parsing times while maintaining correctness.
