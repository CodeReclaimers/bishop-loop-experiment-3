CRITIQUE: The suggestion to use `str.strip()` for whitespace skipping is fundamentally flawed because `str.strip()` removes ALL leading and trailing whitespace, not just from the current position forward. This would break parsing by skipping too much content and misaligning the parse position. Additionally, `str.strip()` is not faster than a simple loop for this use case, especially when only a few characters need skipping.
STEELMAN: A better approach is to replace the manual loop in `_skip_ws` with a more efficient method that uses `str.lstrip()` with the whitespace set, or better yet, a single regex match that advances the position without creating intermediate strings, while still maintaining correctness and performance.
