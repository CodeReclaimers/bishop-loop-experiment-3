Replace the per‑character whitespace‑skipping loop in `parse` with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)`, which jumps straight to the next non‑whitespace character without iterating through each character. This eliminates the overhead of the Python loop on every whitespace block and is equivalent to the accepted “skippy” fix. The same approach can be applied in `_parse_object` and the whitespace skips within it to further cut the call count.
