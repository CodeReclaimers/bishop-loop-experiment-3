Replace every call to `text.find_nonws(pos)` with a simple while‑loop that advances `pos` while `text[pos]` is a whitespace character, i.e., `while pos < len(text) and text[pos] in _WS_CHARS: pos += 1`. This in‑line scanning eliminates the overhead of repeatedly invoking the `find_nonws` function and directly manipulates the index, which is O(1) per skip and thus faster.
