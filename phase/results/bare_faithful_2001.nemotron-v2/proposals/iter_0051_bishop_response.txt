Replace the per‑character whitespace‑skipping loops in `parse`, `_parse_object`, and `_parse_value` with a single call to a pre‑compiled regular‑expression `re.match(r'\s*', text, pos)` that consumes all whitespace at once; this eliminates the Python‑level loop overhead and reduces the number of iterations per character. The regex is compiled once at the top and reused, so parsing whitespace becomes an O(1) operation instead of O(n) iterations.
