Replace the per‑call Python loop that skips whitespace by calling `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` to jump straight to the next non‑whitespace character, which is O(1) instead of O(k) per character. This change only affects the three whitespace‑skipping sections in `parse`, `_parse_object`, and `_parse_array`, eliminating the overhead of looping through each whitespace character.
