Replace the per‑character whitespace skipping loops in `parse` and its helper functions (e.g., `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_number`) with a single `text.find` call that jumps `pos` directly to the next character outside `_WS_CHARS` or the end of a numeric/keyword token.
