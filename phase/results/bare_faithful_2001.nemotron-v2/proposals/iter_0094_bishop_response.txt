Replace the per‑iteration whitespace‑skipping loops in `parse`, `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, and `_parse_array` that use a Python loop over `_WS_CHARS` with a single `str.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` call that jumps to the next non‑whitespace character at once, eliminating the repeated small loops and the overhead of evaluating the set membership each step. This reduces the number of Python‑level iterations from many per‑character checks to one fast C‑level scan per whitespace segment.
