Replace the per‑character whitespace checks in `_skip_ws` and the similarly bounded loops in `_parse_value` and `_parse_object` with a single call to `str.find('{', pos)` or `str.find('[', pos)` that returns the index of the next non‑whitespace character, which moves the work to C‑level for O(k) skipping without Python‑level iteration. This eliminates the repeated set‑lookup and loop overhead while preserving correctness.
