Replace the slicing checks in `_parse_bool` that compare `text[pos:pos+4] == "true"` and `text[pos:pos+5] == "false"` with a single `text.startswith("true", pos)` and `text.startswith("false", pos)` call, which avoids creating substring objects and runs in O(1) time. This change eliminates the overhead of slice creation and direct substring equality testing for boolean literals.
