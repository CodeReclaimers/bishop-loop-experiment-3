CRITIQUE: The suggestion to replace the while loop with a single `text.find` call is unworkable because `text.find` only finds the first occurrence of whitespace characters, not all consecutive whitespace. It would fail to skip over multiple whitespace characters in a row, and would not handle the case where the input ends with whitespace correctly. Additionally, it doesn't account for the fact that the loop is meant to skip a variable number of whitespace characters, not just find one.
