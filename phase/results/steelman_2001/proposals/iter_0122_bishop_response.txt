Replace the character‑by‑character digit checks in `_parse_number` and the similar hex‑escape validation with a single compiled regex that extracts the full numeric or escaped‑escape substring in one pass, then converts the matched text to the appropriate type. This eliminates the per‑character loop overhead and the repeated set‑membership lookups, giving a linear‑time parse with constant‑time tokenisation. The rest of the parser can keep using the same `pos` pointer to continue scanning.
