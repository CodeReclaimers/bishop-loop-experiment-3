Replace the in‑place dictionary updates in `_parse_object` with a list of `(key, value)` tuples that you collect during the parse loop, then construct the final `dict` with a single `dict(tuple)` call instead of repeated `out[key] = value` assignments. This eliminates the overhead of many dictionary key lookups and updates, which is the main bottleneck.
