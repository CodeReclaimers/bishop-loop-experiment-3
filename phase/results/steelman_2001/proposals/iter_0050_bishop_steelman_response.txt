Additionally, it doesn't handle the various number formats correctly, such as hex literals or invalid sequences like `"123."` or `"123e"`.

STEELMAN: A better approach is to use `re.search` with a regex pattern that matches valid JSON number formats in one go, then slice and convert the matched substring.
