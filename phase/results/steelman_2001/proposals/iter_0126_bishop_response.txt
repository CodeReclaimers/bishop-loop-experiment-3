Replace the repeated string concatenation used to build objects and arrays (e.g., `out = out + [v]` and `out = dict(out); out[k] = v`) with in‑place accumulation into a list for the keys/values and then construct the final container once at the end of each parsing phase. This avoids allocating a new string on every iteration, reducing the cost of the `+` operation and allowing the interpreter to perform fewer, larger concatenations.
