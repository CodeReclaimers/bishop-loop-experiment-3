Replace the per‑character loop in _skip_ws with a single call to text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos) that jumps directly to the next non‑whitespace character, eliminating the repeated index increments. This single‑step scan of whitespace is O(1) per segment and is faster than iterating one character at a time. The rest of the parser can continue to use the same position returned by the new find.
