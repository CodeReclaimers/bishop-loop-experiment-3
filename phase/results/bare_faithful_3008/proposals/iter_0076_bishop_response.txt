In the `_skip_ws` function, replace the current while‑loop that checks each character against `_WS_CHARS` with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` that returns the index of the first non‑whitespace character, thereby skipping the entire block in one operation. This eliminates the per‑character checks and reduces the work to O(1) per whitespace block.
