Replace the per‑character whitespace checks in `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, and `_parse_array` with a single call to `_skip_ws`, which uses `str.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` to advance past all whitespace at once, and use that returned index elsewhere instead of looping through each character.
