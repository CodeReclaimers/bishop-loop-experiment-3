Replace the character‑by‑character loop in `_skip_ws` with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)`. This lets Python’s C‑level `find` locate the next non‑whitespace character in one pass, avoiding the overhead of the Python `while` loop that checks each character individually.
