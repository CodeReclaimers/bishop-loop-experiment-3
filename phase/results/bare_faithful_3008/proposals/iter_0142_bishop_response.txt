Replace the repeated while‑loops that skip whitespace with calls to ``text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)`` inside _parse_value, _parse_object, _parse_array and _parse_string, which jumps directly to the next non‑whitespace character instead of checking each character individually.
