Replace the repetitive while‑loop that checks each character with `text[pos] in _DIGIT_CHARS` in `_parse_number` by using `text.find(''.join(_DIGIT_CHARS), pos)` to locate the next non‑digit in one call, then advance `pos` directly to that index. This single‑call find eliminates the per‑iteration membership checks, giving a constant‑time jump over runs of digits. The rest of the number parsing (decimal point, exponent) can stay unchanged.
