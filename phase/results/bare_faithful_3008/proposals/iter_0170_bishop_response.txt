Replace the repeated while‑loops that advance `pos` one character at a time in `_skip_ws`, `_parse_value`, `_parse_object`, `_parse_array`, `_parse_string`, `_parse_bool`, `_parse_null`, `_parse_number`, `_parse_object` and `_parse_array` with a single call to `text.find(''.join(_WS_CHARS), pos)` to locate the next non‑whitespace character in one step, then set `pos` to that index. This eliminates the per‑character loop overhead while preserving correctness because the set `_WS_CHARS` covers all allowed whitespace.
