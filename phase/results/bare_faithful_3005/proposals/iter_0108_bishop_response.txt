Replace the manual character‑by‑character whitespace skip in _skip_ws with a single call to the compiled _WS_PATTERN regex, using _WS_PATTERN.search(text, pos) to return the index of the first non‑whitespace character directly. This leverages the regex engine’s O(k) scanning over the whole whitespace block at once, avoiding the per‑character loop and yielding the same result while improving speed.
