Replace the linear character‑by‑character `_skip_ws` loop with a binary‑search lookup on a pre‑computed list of whitespace indices stored once per parse, so each call jumps straight to the next non‑whitespace character instead of scanning the string. This change reduces the average whitespace‑skipping cost from O(k) per call to O(log w) where k is the number of consecutive whitespace characters and w is the total whitespace count in the input, while preserving the exact position returned.
